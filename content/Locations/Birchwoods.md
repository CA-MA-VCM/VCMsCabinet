> [!quote] Quote
> "Back in the days, spirit nights weren't as strong as the ones we currently experience. The Birch had this catalyst of sorts to enhance the presence of these spirits. It was as if the spirits were infused by it. It's the same reason why the Socialites deeply cherish the forest; it's a gate to the other world, a purgatory." - Calliope Chambers

Compared to the rest of the Forest, the Birchwoods stands as an outlier due to it's sheer spiritual presence. 

While not a lot is confirmed, the presence of the woods is written to have enhanced the spiritual presence across the camp. It's deeply cherished by the Socialites, but even so, the reason behind remains locked behind Vivianne and those in command. 

The Birchwoods have been described to be, ironically, closer to Aspen-trees than they are Birch trees. However, this could be merely a side-effect of the paranoia that tends to follow in wake of the Branchwalkers, causing the forest to seem as if it were watching those who entered. 

### Branchwalker presence

Fittingly, the presence of Branchwalkers is abundantly high in this area. Visiting there, the entire roster of the known Branchwalkers (Pre-Baldur & Freyr) was present. Equally, Amberman refuses to step into the Birchwoods themselves - likely due to their abundant presence.
