On camp grounds, you won't find much when it comes to the paranormal. Perhaps a [[Spirits/index|spirit]], but for any real research you'll have to step outside.

The Crystal Valley appears to hold the largest amount of paranormal activity in the world, but even within the valley there are 'hot spots' of sorts, such as the [[Birchwoods]] or the [[Swamp]]. It's also often theorized that the forest itself is alive in some way, as one singular entity or 'deity'.
