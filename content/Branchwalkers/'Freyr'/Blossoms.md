> [!danger] WARNING
> Blossoms are **highly** dangerous anomalous items. Prolonged usage can cause irreversible, psychological damage. It is advised, when in possession of one, to dispose or return it as soon as possible.

### Description
**Blossoms** are small, palm-sized white flowers that once grew off the body of Freyr. They share striking similarity to the white flowers present across the Swamplands, however hold their own, specific abilities. 


### Anomalous Capabilities
Capabilities and effects of Blossoms tend to change dependent on *how long* a researcher has held onto it.

For **early stages**, researchers are likely to find themselves coming across white petals blowing in the wind. Following them is *strongly* advised, as typically it will mean one of two things:

- There is present danger nearby, and the petals are trying to lead you to safety.
- There is something *hidden* nearby, waiting to be discovered. 

However, using the Blossoms often results in severe, psychological side-effects. These frequently come out as hallucinations. One suffering from these sightings may experience:

- Auditory hallucinations, such as hearing the voices of their loved ones. 
- Visionary hallucinations, where one may witness faceless versions of their loved ones.
   - Note; these hallucinations can be extremely *violent*. Some may witness decayed, rotting versions of their loved ones - or those moments before their deaths. 
- Brief possession; holders may find themselves acting as a mouthpiece for a higher-force - even if they don't intend to speak.

For these reasons, holding onto a Blossom is *strongly* unrecommended. Prolonged usage can result in these effects worsening and becoming **permanent**. 

> [!danger] CLASSIFIED
> All information documented below is strictly **classified**. Releasing this information may be grounds for warnings, demotions or exile.

**Late-stage** holders of Blossoms are to be deemed unable to be saved. It is, at this point, that all outside forces have no significant impact on weakening or subduing their hallucinations; there is nothing you can do to help them. 

However, in place of these side-effects, one may come across visions of the future. These visions, however, are extremely violent. Viewers may find themselves being attacked by their hallucinations.

Visions of the future have included:
- Early sightings of Pyre, weeks before his appearance during the Spore-nights. 
- Visions of Sackhead, equally weeks before his appearance in the Ignition Front's mines.

However, alongside these violent visions, one may begin to witness sightings of [[The Crooked Shaman]] in both conscious and unconscious states. Temporary possession develops to a point where limbs can be momentarily controlled against one's will.
