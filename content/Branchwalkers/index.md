Branchwalkers are guardians of the forest made out of trees, although they were formerly human. Each of them is made out of a different type of wood, and has different behaviour to go along with them. They work alongside the [[Bushman]], and are known to be able to write if you hand them a pen and paper. This doesn't mean they always will, however.
