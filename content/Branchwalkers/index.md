**Branchwalkers** are tree-like entities who work alongside the [[Bushman]] in protecting the forest. Once human, these beings now roam the forest under little volition of their own, instead following the commands of the Bushman and the Forest Spirit. They are currently the strongest known entity produced by The Forest, and as a result come in few numbers.

# Physical Overview

Branchwalkers come in a variety of different shapes and sizes, with few maintaining a more humanoid appearance whereas others may appear as a grotesque mutation of their original being. 

Their most notable traits are their wood-like skin, all of which resemble different species of trees; some examples include Aspen, Oak, Pine and Ebony. As a result, all Branchwalkers have shown to sprout roots and branches from their body, of which are often mistaken for horns. 

On occasion, Branchwalkers can be seen with floral growths sprouting out from their body in the form of moss, flowers and even fruit. This, however, tends to be a result of their environment, giving evidence that floral life can grow off their bodies. 

Alongside this, Branchwalkers can be recognised by their luminescent, glowing eyes. A majority of Branchwalkers only have one eye, however some have been shown to upwards of two or more.

On rare occasions, their forms can include new extremities, limbs and organs such as additional arms and over-extended joints. 

# Behaviour

On first glance, one can misjudge Branchwalkers to be skittish in nature. They rarely stay in one-spot and oftentimes hide themselves away from the public eye; it is only, through enough effort, can one spot a Branchwalker in their natural habitat. Even then, appearances are infrequent and brief.

Having once been human, Branchwalkers hold onto fragments of their former personality and memories. For example [['Freyr']], who was previously a child before being transformed, appears much kinder and playful compared to it's counterparts. In some circumstances, Branchwalkers have shown themselves in order to guide Campers away from active threats such as the [[Amberman]].

Alongside their personality, their knowledge and I.Q. carries over. Branchwalkers have been shown to possess the ability to both read and write in response to being given notes.

Despite being unable to speak, Branchwalkers have - on rare occasion - produced sounds similar to that of laughter, grunts, cries and roars. These, oftentimes, are individualistic to each Branchwalker's personalities, with each one making their own unique sound. 

Finally, Branchwalkers **can be appeased** through the act of gift-giving. One can offer a mixture of items such as trinkets and grown food. However, the most widely-accepted gift tends to be jewellery such as necklaces and bracelets.

When prowling, a Branchwalker's behaviour can be broken down into two modes; **Lurking** and **Hunting.**

## Lurking.

When Lurking, Branchwalkers will rarely show themselves to the public eye. Rather, much in the name, they will watch from the backlines in shrubbery or behind trees. Rarely, if ever, will they show themselves in full to a nearby witness.

This phase acts as an in-between for their neutral and hostile states. If you catch yourself being watched by a Branchwalker, there's a high chance it's surveying you for any possible threats. 

If, in the event that it perceives you to be a threat or to be in danger, it will oftentimes show itself to ward you away. It is **strongly** recommended to do so; refusal to cooperate may result in it becoming hostile.

## Hunting.

Hunting begins once a Branchwalker has been given full warranty to attack an individual. This happens **as a result of one committing transgressions against The Forest** and, much like the Bushman, it will put full effort into taking it's due. 

In this phase, Branchwalkers lose all remnants of their former personality. They devolve entirely into weapons of war, and will only exit this state of behaviour once their perceived threat has been eliminated. It is, during this phase, that Branchwalkers will often come out from to directly attack their victim instead. 

# Anomalous Capabilities

Branchwalkers possess a wide array of supernatural capabilities that assist them in protecting The Forest.

In rare circumstances, Branchwalkers may hold their own unique abilities. This can include powers such as: producing hallucinations, rotting of organic life and the ability to communicate with animals. 

Below are a list of capabilities recognised across all forms of Branchwalkers.

### Supernatural Durability.

Being made of bark and wood, Branchwalkers have an innately tough exterior. Their body is durable enough to withstand live-rounds from shotguns and assault rifles and even intense thrashing from the Amberman. 

Both blunt and sharp attacks have proved very little effect to their body, with the impact being near-negligible. 

### Regenerative capabilities.

When in the presence of sunlight, Branchwalkers possess the ability to regenerate their wounds. This process follows a similar cycle to that of Photosynthesis. 

When healing, present wounds such as cracks and cuts can be seen folding inwards into a close. This process, however, is not instantaneous; it can take upwards from hours to days for a Branchwalker to fully recover from it's wounds. 

### Phasing.

Similarly to the Bushman, Branchwalkers are able to "phase" out from our perception by accessing flora such as shrubbery and bushes. *However*, this ability is far better crafted than the Bushman's, with them being able to root themselves into the ground and disappear from seemingly thin-air. They can equally reappear near-instantaneously, often leading many to misjudge this ability for teleportation. 

### Fear-manipulation.

Branchwalkers possess the unique ability to inflict bouts of paranoia and anxiety to nearby individuals as a means of deterrent. This often time comes in the form of,

- The sensation of being watched. 
- Shortness of breath.
- Restlessness.
- Shaking, trembling or sweating.

The full capability of this is unknown. However, it has been shown to cause "Anxiety-Induced Relaxation", a unique, rare case where the body short-circuits itself from the adrenaline to go into a state of pure relaxation. 

### Omniscience.

Similar to the Bushman, Branchwalkers possess the capability to watch people through the natural flora of The Forest. Transgressions made against nature are near-immediately picked up, causing the lurking Branchwalkers to actively hone-in on their target. 

# Counters

Counters to a Branchwalker frequently overlap with the Bushman's; **do not, under any circumstances, cause any transgressions against it.**

- Do not aggravate Branchwalkers. 
- When told to leave an area, do as such.
- Do not harm the wildlife or the surrounding nature.

One can, however, put themselves in favour to the Branchwalkers by offering gifts.
