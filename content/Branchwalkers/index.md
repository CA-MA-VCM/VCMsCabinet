*A branchwalker of oak make, dubbed [['Hlodyn']].*

Branchwalkers are guardians of the forest made out of trees, although they were formerly human. Each of them is made out of a different type of wood, and has different behaviour to go along with them. They work alongside the [[Bushman]].
