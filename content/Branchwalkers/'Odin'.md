Odin, or the "Birchwalker", is one of the main, most well known [[Branchwalkers/index|Branchwalker]] that both the A.R.C. and camp has seen. Knowledge of this one seems to extend across all faces, with most knowing of it's existence. It's been primarily recognized for its "assistance" with helping Campers flee from danger. However, this assistance mainly comes in the form of scaring Campers into running away before whatever danger might find them. 

As for physical form, Odin is most recognisable by it's Birch-make, extra pair of arms and it's two distinct yellow and orange glowing eyes. It appears to be one of the *bulkier* Branchwalkers out of the roster, being able to withstand intensive thrashings from the Amberman. 

The crow that rests on one of it's horns seems to be quite an interesting case. Numerous times, it's come over to groups of people moments before Odin himself arrives. Odds are that Odin can "use" this crow to scout out regions before arriving there himself.

Despite it's apparent "friendliness", Odin has been seen multiple times to attack campers during a Bush-hunt, most notably during the skirmish Marty hosted against the Bushman. During this time, it was openly attacking every and all campers that came close to it. Even those with a personal, positive reputation with the Forest were put at risk. 

It is, also, the first Branchwalker to showcase their innate ability to "heal" from wounds. This pseudo-regeneration comes out in the form of "short-bursts", where cracks and splinters appeared to rather sharply snap close on themselves. Despite this, the wounds Odin sustained from the Amberman attack took several days to heal.
