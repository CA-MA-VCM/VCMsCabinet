Welcome to the ARC wiki! This is part of a new effort to improve our documentation, and make it easier for newer members to participate. This wiki is still a work in progress, but is intended to hold all of our information and make it easily available. If you'd like to [[Contributing Guide|contribute, see here.]]

We also ask of you to not share this wiki with people outside of ARC, even OOCly. We'd like to keep the risk of metagaming to a minimum. If you are removed from the club, please refrain from using the website, as you do not ICly have access to the club's documents.

# How-to

Over on the left, you'll find a tab of folders and files. Folders themselves are also files. For example, you can click on "Branchwalkers" itself rather than any of the underlying files to get an explanation of what a Branchwalker is.

On the top left, you have a search bar. This search bar also combs through all the contents of the articles, including all field logs posted here. Looking up "Bushman" for example, will retrieve the Bushman article as well as any field log that references him.
