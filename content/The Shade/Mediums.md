**Medium** is the classification for a rare group of people who have permanent access to [[The Shade/index|The Shade]]. 

Those in this state appear to have full ability to converse and interact with the incorporeal realm and, in rare circumstances, wield it. 

## Known Medium-associated Abilities

No Medium shares the same powers. Each ability seems personal to their user and as a result, no Medium is the same for the next.
### Psychic / Telekinetic Abilities 

These forms of powers have been showcased through Mothlight, an individual working closely alongside the White Masks. 

These abilities allow him to access the deepest corner's of people's minds, rekindling old memories and trauma. Those with a weak-enough mental fortitude place themselves in danger of being possessed by him. 

Alongside this, he has showcased the ability to telekinetically lift and throw objects. This ability is *heavily* trained, as seen through his ability to vibrate his cleaver fast enough to set it alight. 

### Clairvoyance

This ability pertains strictly to an individual known as "The Oracle". Not much is known about the extent of their ability, but they have been able to detect "spies" within the camp and foretell some aspects of the future. 

### Clone-creation

An ability used by Vivianne. She can, temporarily, create several visages of her own self. 

This was most recognisable during a skirmish between Campers and the Ignition Front, where she created several dozen copies of herself to force the armed militia to retreat. 

This, however, has shown to have *severe* drawbacks. Creating such a large number of clones left her in an extremely weakened and sickened state.

## Obtaining Medium-hood

There is, currently, no known way to obtain this state. As of now, no campers have attained Medium-hood asides from Vivianne, who has been on Camp Grounds for several years. 

However, as far as knowledge goes, one must *damage* their soul enough to the point where they cannot return from The Shade, leaving them perpetually in it. 

The process is likely to be dangerous and life-threatening. By no means should anyone try to obtain this.
