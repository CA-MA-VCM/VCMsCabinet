**Blueshrooms** are a form of [[The Shade/index|Shades]], often characterised by their Mushroom-like shape and luminescent blue glow. They are, as of now, the only way for non-mediums to access the shade.

When consumed, Blueshrooms temporarily allow one to access the Shade, offering them the ability to both see and interact with the incorporeal world. Effects tend to last for upwards of an hour and are dependant on how much is consumed. 

## Harvesting Blueshrooms

Blueshrooms grow naturally in Spirit-abundant areas. They tend to grow in patches ranging from one to three fruiting bodies. 

Harvesting them is simple. Taking a hold of it by the stem, one can cut (or pull) from the base it's growing out of. Try to get as close to it's original growth as possible to harvest it's full potential.

Seemingly, Blueshrooms don't appear to expire, so the need to refrigerate them is unnecessary. It is still recommended to store them in a container of your choice to minimise cross-contamination. 

## Side effects

Once the effects wear off, Blueshrooms oftentimes induce sickness to the user. Whether it's due to natural toxicity or the sudden shift to the Shade is unconfirmed. However, one must remain cautious when consuming them as too many can result in death. 

Alongside this, the consumption of Blueshrooms can garner the attention of certain Entities such as Nail, which can place users in long-term danger. Equally, the usage of Blueshrooms may attract more spirits to the surrounding area.

 >[!danger] WARNING
> When taking Blueshrooms, one must be absolutely clear on the side-effects present. There are significant risks behind taking them and as a result, people must be absolutely certain on their use.
