**The Shade** is the thin veil that rests between life and death. Those who are able to access this thin line have the ability to interact with both life and death. 

In this state, one has the ability to both physically interact with and witness the incorporeal world. However this isn't a boon without drawbacks. **Not all spirits are friendly**, and as a result, they may be able to physically harm or even kill you. 

## Reaching The Shade

Accessing the Shade is complicated. One must either be a [[Mediums|Medium]], or, make use of the various **Shades** around the Crystal Valley to temporarily access the veil. However, these rarely come without drawbacks, of which oftentimes condemns the user's physical and/or mental wellbeing. The most common method of accessing the shade is through [[Blueshrooms]].
