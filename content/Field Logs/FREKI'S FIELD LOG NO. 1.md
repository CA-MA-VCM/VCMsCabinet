**"HERE COMES THE ROOSTER."**
*"Army green ain't no safe bet. These boys learned it the hard way."*

**CONDITIONS**
> **TIME:** Pretty late into the night, occurred during the A.R.C in character recruitment.
> **LOCATION:** On the campgrounds outside the Cafeteria and beside cabins.

**ANOMALIES, ENTITIES, & MISC.**
> Ghost dubbed, 'Devil Dog', by FREKI during the encounter.

**CONTEXT**
> "I think this ghost was the entity various others had encountered a couple weeks back, the descriptions that were given to me seem to match it. Spores were moderate, just our luck too, 'cause we were hosting a recruitment with a couple of new people. They got to see firsthand what we do. 
> 
> Devil Dog appears to be from the early nineteen-hundreds gauging what he was wearing, which makes me curious as to how he got his hands on a spirit box. It is also safe to assume that he is a World War One soldier and served in the United States Military as a Marine, calling his squad 'Devil Dogs' and shouting about the final war."

**ENCOUNTER**
> "Arriving at the camp our group had joined a small crowd of campers outside the infirmary to make sure everyone was staying safe, before various witnesses were reporting sightings of a ghost, Nail specifically. We rushed towards the direction they were giving us only to be met, not by Nail, but a new ghost that was immediately hostile and irritated. Notable features of the entity being as follows,"
> 
> - A deformed skull and face, grotesque eyes covering most of its head.
> - Early nineteen-hundreds military attire and tools
> - A spirit-box, seemingly an incredibly old model. Possibly a prototype?"

> "Devil Dog is completely convinced that we, the campers, are ghosts. No amount of reasoning or attempts to communicate would deter this belief. He also believes that he is in fact, not dead, and is searching for his lost 'comrades'. He also appears to have some form of ability to interact with physical objects, completely demolishing the inside of a camper's cabin and ripping a light fixture from the ground.

> "I managed to be able to get alone with the entity inside the cabin he had destroyed. He seemed to calm down somewhat when I showed him my father's dog tags, however quickly became enraged when asking what war they were from. I was lucky he was not able to physically touch me and just fling objects around me. Devil Dog had also stated that he researched and investigated ghosts himself, however, would not elaborate as to how long or why. Unfortunately, he quickly left short after, commanding us to not follow him before he became incorporeal."

**NOTES**
> "Multiple questions first come to mind. Why is a U.S. Marine in Camp Astrid? A World War One Marine at that. Who are his brothers at arms? What were they doing here? How did he get a spirit-box? It's.. quite strange that *he* himself was apparently a ghost-hunter. It is also odd that a ghost as strong as him is so oblivious to his circumstances.
> 
> It is possible that we may never learn about Devil Dog's past due to his erratic and uncooperative nature. I am sure that, if the chances were to ever arise, he would hurt and likely kill a camper out of what he would perceive as self-defense. I recommend staying far away from him during high Spore events if he were to show himself.
> 
> I'd like to conduct more research with Devil Dog if the chances ever arise again."
