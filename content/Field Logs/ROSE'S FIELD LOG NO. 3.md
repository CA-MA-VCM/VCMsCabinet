## __AN INTERACTION WITH ANGELA.__

` 10/14 - AFTERNOON - THE BEACH`
`WITNESSES: Evelyn, Cal, Kate, Leila, Sam`  

### Before the encounter
> It was just a regular afternoon in Camp, with many people hanging out at the beach. Everyone seemed pretty positive/happy in the surrounding area, with many even getting some free Ice cream. There is not much else to comment on from before this interaction happened.

### The Interaction.
> Angela, a ghost which I have not yet interacted with showed up behind one of the cabins on the beach. A crowd quickly gathered around her, which at this point that is something I've come to expect. One of the campers that were around, Vic, asked who they were. They said that their name was ***Angela*** and we needed to ***'Remember her name, and remember it well.'***. I can already say in comparison to my other interactions with ghosts, Angela seemed a lot more... straight forward should we say.
> 
> Evelyn enquired on what they were doing here, which they had responded that there was some disturbances in the camp, and they wanted explanations. Angela asked Evelyn to explain further, however other campers that were watching alongside us kept interrupting. Luckily Eve was able to mention a few different things, including mentioning the Amber man, which they found very interested by. Angela also took notice of the fact Evelyn said blue, however did not comment on it, instead turning her attention to the other campers.

### Frustration with Campers
> From this point, it felt like the interaction went downhill. To summarise it, multiple camp members were either not threatened by Angela, or continued to be disrespectful in one aspect or another. Angela tried being more respectful, hoping that the campers would follow suit, however they did not. Not much else of importance came from a lot of this interaction.
> 
> However before leaving the crowd, she did tell Arnie, and to the crowd in general that our next day could be our last, so we should live our lives with that notion. A camper then asked if she had lost something, which seemed to trigger something inside of her, with her leaving the crowd shortly after that.

### Aftermath/Conclusion.
> After this interaction, most campers went back to talking with one another. However some campers warned other ones to be more careful and not instigate things with the ghosts. I think this interaction had startled more people then the other interactions I had seen.
> 
> Still, questions are left after that interaction. How much do the ghosts know about the entities that live among us in Camp Astrid? And why did someone asking her if she lost something trigger her to leave?
