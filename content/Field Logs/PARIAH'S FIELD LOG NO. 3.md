## MEETING THE FOREST 

__*PREFACE*__
> "Before we get to business, I want to praise everyone for how they handled that investigation."

> "I'm aware that it was scary; but I have to acknowledge how well you all managed to handle yourselves during times of possible danger. Everyone managed to remain respectful and diligent, and that has to be acknowledged."

> "To put it simply: Good job. I'm really happy with the standard we have set for ourselves."

__*SETUP*__
> “We had made preparations a few days in advance for what was coming. The lot of us, though mainly Catherine, collected an amass of gifts and offerings to give to the Branchwalkers and the Bushman. If we wanted to up our relations, this was the best way to go about it. We gathered an assortment of:"
> - Bonemeal 
> - Potting soil
> - Fertilizer
> - Foodstuffs including Trail mix and Beef Jerky 
> - and Medical Supplies.”

> “Although we wanted to better our relations, we also wanted to show our support where we could. After all, Bushman and the Branchwalkers were taking the fight themselves, so we should work from the sidelines the best we can to help them. If they fall, we fall too. They're our last hope of keeping both ourselves and other Campers safe when the time calls.” 

__*THE JOURNEY*__
> “The trip there was quite simple. We cut through the Swamps from the Lighthouse and broke into the Birch Forest. The swamp, at the time, yielded nothing. It was quiet unlike my last visit, where it seemed near *life-threatening* to enter.” 

> “For a moment, the Forest went deafeningly silent. At first, I was afraid that they had moved on. Yet, soon enough, Odin’s crow flew by, and the forest grew louder.” 

> “I should note for for those unaware; the ‘ambience’ of the Forest seems to follow that of the Branchwalkers, and even maybe even the Bushman himself too. In future, take note of the Forest’s noises. You may not be as alone as you once thought.” 

> “Once we caught sight of the crow, we followed it further into the Birchwoods - deep enough where we broke back into the Swamp and settled near some derelict housing."

![[pariah_log_three_img1_path.png]]

__*CABIN-SETUP*__
> "We gathered up around a lit fire. Our best move was to stick together and *wait*. We couldn't see further than maybe ten-feet in front of us, the fog covered everything. I have my own curiosities about that, it seems awfully coincidental to have it roll in after following Odin's crow into the Swamp."

> "Regardless, we kept eyes on all angles. There was, at first, no possible way for a Branchwalker to sneak up on us. That was, until, we started hearing this odd *laughter* around us. Apparently word has it that this laughter followed a specific Branchwalker that seemed to *"flicker"* about. No active harm was caused, but it certainly raised tension across the party." 

> "It wasn't long before Morgan and I saw something in the fog. I took the initiative to move forward, and I was able to *just* about notice Hlodyn. At the time, I wasn't sure *what* I was looking at, but I know now. Regardless, looking back on it this felt particularily off. Having a Branchwalker so *built* for combat such as Hlodyn was quite a worrying sight. I'm just glad that everyone wasn't affected." 

__*POST-ANNOUNCEMENT*__
> "After letting the group know about the Branchwalkers being here, everything went downhill. Both at the time, and looking back on it, I realised how *bad* of an idea that was to announce it so loudly. It might have just been poor timing, but my voice felt *weaker*. Words from then-on didn't quite come out as I wanted them." 

> "After that, both myself and the entire group noticed Odin's presence at the far end of where we had set up. I took the chance that we had to step out from base and make my way over. The moment I did, however, Odin vanished, leaving Heimdall in his place."

> "It was almost nostalgic to see him poking out from a bush, as odd as that sounds. The anxiety and fear that surrounded him came back - only weaker, this time. Perhaps I had gotten used to it."

> "I only managed a few steps forward before hearing a crunch behind me. At the time, I wasn't aware of what it *really* was. I was tunnel-visioned on what was ahead. Apparently, Odin had appeared behind me. What that crunch was -- I have no idea."

> "Knowing him, however, it may have been a warning. One that I ignored. I treaded closer a final time, which warrented Heimdall to slam back into the tree behind them. The noise was loud enough to feel as if it'd shake the whole damn woods down. Their message was clear, though. We had overstayed our visit."

__*LEAVING*__
> "Hurridly, we all left the scene. We didn't give ourselves proper time to actually deposit the offerings properly; we could only leave them on the ground a few feet away from the cabin we had hunkered down in." 

> "It wasn't long before Bushman showed up, too. Though, not brandishing arms as he normally would. Instead, he simply followed us back to the Camp, as if he were making sure we properly made it home. It was, suprisingly, a clean evacuation. I had expected more to go wrong, but we all got out scot-free (apart for Catherine, whom had cut her hand on the way back).

## KNOWN BRANCHWALKERS

__*PREFACE*__
> "This is going to be short."

> "As most are aware, my own experiences with Branchwalkers has been wildly different to the average Camper's. Do not take my words as saying "these ones are safe". Always take caution when approaching Branchwalkers, or any entities for that matter." 

> "Regardless, here's my documented research as of far. If you have any more to add, let me know in person and I'll be sure to update this."

### __*ODIN*__
> "Odin, or the "Birchwalker", is one of the main, most well known Branchwalkers that both the A.R.C. and camp has seen. Knowledge of this one seems to extend across all faces, with most knowing of it's existence. It's been primarily recognized for its "assistance" with helping Campers flee from danger. However, this assistance mainly comes in the form of scaring Campers into running away before whatever danger might find them. They were recently severely injured during the skirmish between Bushman and Amberman and is currently healing."

> "I should also mention it's one of the more *bulkier* Branchwalkers. It has multiple arms sprouting from it's back - somewhere around four in total. We're not sure if these arms follow the same capabilities as it's primary ones, but we'll just have to see."

> "The crow that rests on one of it's horns seems to be quite an interesting case. Numerous times, it's come over to groups of people moments before Odin himself arrives. Odds are that Odin can "*use*" this crow to scout out regions before arriving there himself. The crow itself seems... *mortal*. I write this in mention that it seems to have a need to eat, with myself having a first-hand sighting of it eating a walnut."

> "As for dangers, this one seems to be friendly. *To* a degree. During the Bush-hunt hosted by Marty, this was the *sole* Branchwalker that took arms (pun intended) against the Campers. This was my first time trying the "Solace the Forest" phrase, but it gave no response. It may have been attacking wildly at all forms of life. Overall, however, I wouldn't place great caution onto them. Still, keep your wits about you."

### __*HEIMDALL*__
> "This one is complicated."

> "You'll recognize this one by it's large, spindly branch that stretches up nearly the same length as it's body. It's of dark-wooden make. Something like spruce, perhaps? I've never been close enough to find out. You'll be able to notice it once you see the branch sticking out from a bush." 

> "First of all, *my* interactions with it has been overall positive. For everyone else, however, it's been bad. Apparently this one has access to Mental-Altering abilities, which tracks from both my experiences and other's. I'm unsure of the extent to these abilities, but it's enough to send you into a panic attack. At one point, it stressed me out so much that I simply *couldn't* get stressed anymore. Whether that was intentional, or not, I'm unsure. Though, I'm liable to agree that it did so in order to calm me down, *as* contradictory as that sounds."

> "I did a bit of reading and checking in with others - apparently they've been present in Shared Dreams, before. This wouldn't be unsuprising. Afterall, with a being that can alter minds, it wouldn't be a far stretch to see them be able to alter dreams as well." 

> "Finally, if that weren't enough, Heimdall seems god-awful strong. Just from the records heard in the Shared Dreams and how he nearly shook-apart the Forest last night, I wouldn't be suprised if he was one of the strongest out of the bunch." 

> "I don't know how to rank this one. I *would* say to take caution, but I'm not sure of it's motives given my-own position."

### __*HAFGUFA*__
> "Hafgufa, named after the Nordic Sea Monster, is a Branchwalker of Birch-make that is covered in a mesh of fishing line, nets and sea-litter. The bark seems quite... worn for wear. It looks closer to that of bleached drift-wood you would see washed up on shore." 

> "As far as abilities and personality go... This one is quite hard to put a pin on. We saw it briefly during the A.R.C. Recruitment a few weeks ago. It was following us near the end, and seemed to almost ~~chase~~ shephard us back to camp. I wouldn't be surprised if it was acting similar to Odin and making sure we weren't in dangers way."

> "This one seems to have quite the habit for appearing and dissapearing. It one of the more mobile ones, oftentimes not wanting to stay in place. Perhaps it's just skittish?"

> "Danger-wise, I'd say it's safer to interact with than Odin. Just don't push it's boundaries, sometimes people don't like to be bothered."

![[hafgufa1.png|500]]

### __*HLODYN*__
> "Hlodyn, or Fjord if you wanna' pronounce it easier, is quite a simple case. It's a two-horned Branchwalker of Oak-make."

> "I only ran into this one quite recently. As far as I know, it retains the typical powers as most Branchwalkers (such as dissapearing and reappearing on whim). It's quite the intimidating sight, possibly due to it's rather-large horns compared to that of the others. I should also mention it's *roots*. Compared to the *blunter* roots that other Branchwalkers have, these ones are *notoriously* sharp. I wouldn't be surprised if, in tandem with it's bull-like horns, rammed into it's opponents in an attempt to skewer them. It sounds like quite the brutal method."

> "This is, however, possibly the closest image to what I first imagined when I heard the name '*Branchwalker*'. If you want to figure out of something is a Branchwalker, compare it to this one as a basis."

> "If anyone has greater details on this one's abilities, personality or habits, *do* let me know."

> "Retain caution for now."

### __*LOKI*__
> "This is one I only became aware of as-of recent." 

> "Named after the Nordic God of Trickery and Mischief, this one appears to be quite the... *playful* one. Compared to other Branchwalkers, it seems to have quite the tendancy to get up-close behind people and appear behind them. Their appearances only stay for a second at best. This one, compared to all others, seem's to have quite a grasp on it's innate ability to disappear and reappear." 

> "Not to mention, this one seems to be *quite* loud. When it originally appeared at the Swamp-Cabins last night, the sound of gravelly laughter seemed to follow it. Or, that's at least how it was described. Perhaps it's grown cocky and mischievous after realizing it has such an ability to jump in and out of harms way." 

> "Danger? Low. Just expect to be scared left-and-right when it's about. As always, though, retain caution around this one just as much as the rest."
