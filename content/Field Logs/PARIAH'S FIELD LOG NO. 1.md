## __NAIL SIGHTING AT THE STONEHENGE__
*DATE*: 10/02/87
*TIME*: ~12:00am and onwards.
*LOCATION*: Stonehenge site at the Meadows.
*WITNESS(ES)*: Calliope Chambers, Evelyn, Lisa and other Non-A.R.C. campers/counselors.

*__PHENOMENON(S)__* 
- Sound 
- Ghost-sighting
- Gate-response

__*WHAT HAPPENED?*__
> "I arrived to the Stonehenges on my own accord. I originally planned to conduct my own research, only to find a group of campers and stray counselors sat about and talking. I tried to warn them of the possible dangers of remaining there, but they refused to move."

> "Deciding to stand-by, I took the time to gloss over the structure of the Stonehenge once more. Although I made no new discoveries, something else stood out compared to my usual visits; the sound of footsteps."

> "Checking in with the other Campers, no one else was able to catch the sound. I originally brushed it off as a result of poor sleep, yet I began to question that the second time the sounds occurred. Something was watching us; and I could feel it. I tried to spot whatever it was in the surrounding trees, yet was unable to see anything. Not until a moments after."

> "Soon enough, the source of the footsteps were revealed. Marching across the cliff-face and over to the end of the Stonehenge was Nail himself.  Before I had time to react, all the pillars in the Stonehenge burst into a bright, white light the moment he got close enough. We evacuated soon after."

*__CONDITIONS__*
> "Night time."

> "There was a relatively large gathering of people; around seven-to-eight with myself included." 

> "Unsure of what stage of the Lunar cycle it was."

*__REACTIONS__:*
> "Most panicked, a fair response given the notoriety Nail carried. Though, given the events from both Nail's appearance during ARC's Ghost Hunt and his recent reappearance, he was of no *active* danger unless we had consumed Blueshrooms. Knowing this, I took the risk of being caught to try and talk to him."

> "**Results were unsuccessful.** I was caught soon after, and everything went dark. I'm unsure of how long I was out for - nor do I know what happened. I can only assume, given how Evelyn was caught prior herself, that I was surrounded by Nail's smog of ash. I awoke with no injuries, only a persistent headache that carried on for the remainder of the evening and the following day. I wasn't far from where I was caught -- perhaps only a couple dozen feet away. Returning to the site yielded no new results."

*__THEORIES AND NOTES__*
> "First, I want to mention the glow that burst out from the Stonehenges. Typically, as far as witness and sourced evidence goes, the Stonehenges light up in a **Blue** glow once activated. This is particularly common across all spiritual entities. *This* time, however, it glowed white. I'm unsure if it was intentional or not from Nail, nor do I want to jump-the-gun and assume it's the opening process of the Gate. The glow lasted no more than **ten seconds** before disappearing. My current hypothesis is that the White-glow may be an antithesis to the blue glow we commonly seen. This is only backed, however, by knowledge of Nail's hatred for other spirits and the possibility that these Gates may not be one-way. More evidence is required if we want to bring this anywhere near theory-level." 

> "Second, I can't help but question *why* his appearance was so late. Although we couldn't see him, ~~we~~ I could hear his footsteps. Something must have changed to cause all those present on-site to be able to see Nail in his entirety. This wasn't long after the Shade Grove was burnt; perhaps there were still some lingering spores in the air?"

> "For now, I advise all those wanting to investigate the Gates to take caution. Although you may not be at a mortal risk to Nail, what may follow is **not** pleasant. Avoid taking Blueshrooms when on-site, unless absolutely necessary."
