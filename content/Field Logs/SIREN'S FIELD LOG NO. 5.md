**Location:**

> The field party event.

**Witnesses:**

> No one in ARC witnessed this besides myself.

**Phenomenon:**

> Possession.

**What Happened:**

> I was hanging out at the field party, when my friend Devoria started acting strange. Moving different, quiet, different speech pattern— I immediately knew what was going on. This was ANGELA. I stayed beside her the whole time, pulling her aside to talk to her alone. I told her I knew it was her, but she denied it and said she was Dev. Angela has been rekindling with me after The Front encounter at the lighthouse. **Angela**, still Dev, proceeded to go and drink two cartons of milk off of the counter. Apparently it had been awhile since **Angela** had milk and she really missed it. After this, she was speaking to Dev’s boyfriend Yannis, before holding onto the table, breathing hard, and then passing out onto the ground. I told him to stay calm, I knew what was happening. We took her to the infirmary, she stayed out cold for a little while, lying on the bed with twitching hands and an intense sweat. She then shot up, sobbing and panicking. Similar to what happened to me, she was holding onto Yannis as she sobbed. She had seen **Angela’s Memories**, just as I did when it happened to me. She was explaining the memory of when Angela was shot, as well as the memory of a head rolling to her feet when Via was killed. She explained how Angela used to be so happy before everything. All of this I knew very well, and helped her go through. As I was there comforting her, I heard an audible **“BOO.”** In my head. Angela seems to have returned to my mind, and our connection is back. She seems to be more in control now. I’m glad that we could rekindle. I missed her, honestly. It felt lonely. I helped Dev back to the cabin, and she fell asleep. 

**Reactions:**

> I wasn’t surprised. Yannis seemed to have been handling it well, but was not expecting it until I told him. Dev once the possession was over was devastated, panicking and crying. She wanted us with her, she was trying to ground, but was violently going through Angela’s memories, saying things like: *“She was so happy.” “Her intestines were falling out.” “I saw a head roll.”*

**Theory or Notes:**

> Angela is becoming more unstable but more in control. She won’t be as patient or kind, and I’ve noticed her being almost more sassy or prankster ish. Making deals with her or getting her help won’t be as easy. We will continue to have a connection, but I think she has a better handle on her memories involving me. She can still talk to me, but I won’t get as intense flashes of memories as I did before. She’s still there to help me, as I am to her. I trust she’ll show me things if she thinks I need to see them.

That’s all from me,
