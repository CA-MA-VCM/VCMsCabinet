***"NEW-FACES"***
"It's getting busier -- stranger. Things aren't adding up."

__**CONDITIONS**__
> **TIME:** Seven P.M leading into the later hours of the night. 
> 
> **LOCATION:** Swamp Stonehenge

__**ANOMALIES, ABNORMALITIES, ENTITIES OR OTHER**__
> - Auditory anomalies
> - Branchwalker sightings

## RUN DOWN
__**LEAD-UP**__
> "It's been a while since I've written. It feels good to be back." 

> "Crow and myself were hosting a recruitment for a couple new faces. For the majority of the host, it went according to plan. That is, however, until we decided to conduct a *Field Test* for the new recruits. We took 'em down to the Stonehenge in the swamp, wanted to see if any of them came up with some ideas about what they were seeing." 

> "If the two of you are reading this; good job. I have to say, I was well-impressed by your performance there." 

__**START OF ANOMALOUS INTERACTIONS**__
> "We had stayed around the Henges for, say, several minutes. No more than fifteen. Myself, and a couple others, briefly caught wind of the sound of footsteps **[1]** marching around us. This has, as far as I'm aware, always been present around the Stonehenges. Perhaps sound from the realm beyond transferred over to here -- I'm not sure. Regardless, we let the footsteps sound out three times before making our leave back to Grave's cabin."

> "It wasn't long, however, until a Branchwalker sprouted from the ground. It was a completely new face - one I didn't recognise. Compared to the other Branchwalkers, this one is much, *much* larger than the rest - probably standing twice as tall as the rest (asides from Heimdall). It's appearance was, to be frank, horrifying. It came out flailing it's arms wildly as if trying to strike at us - albeit none of it's movements were in range of affecting us."

> "I, however, was split from the group upon it's appearance. Whether it took that into account or not, it turned over to face me *first*, treading over closer than any other Branchwalker has done before **[2]**. I tried to... *placate* the Branchwalker by affirming that none of us were there to do any harm, but I don't think it cared. And, what really took the cake for this sighting, was how it decided to *pick me up* **[3]** and drop me off by the other researchers. Only after that it pointed off to beyond the swamplands and back to the cabin we came from."

> "I'm... *unsure* if anyone else picked up on this, but I could distinctly hear the sound of what *seemed* to be crying. I'm not sure what the cause of it was, but it was vaguely present during the time of the Branchwalkers arrival."

__**NOTES & THEORIES**__
> "As mentioned before, I have growing belief that the footsteps sounding out around the Henges are related to spirits, of those of the realm Beyond. It certainly isn't the Branchwalkers. This has only occurred around the Henges, as far as my experiences have gone." 

> "As for the *Branchwalker* though... putting it in the nicest way possible, it's a bit of a pushover. I think it came out of the ground trying to scare us off with these big flailing motions, but on realising we had been split it took the more peaceful route. It's certainly one of the friendlier Branchwalkers out there and equally seems to be the most comfortable one when coming close to the Campers. As for it's traits... it's strong. Of course, *all* Branchwalkers seem to have a level of superhuman strength and durability to them, but this one picked me up with ease with just one hand."

> "Given how we're now aware of how Branchwalkers were once human, we (or I, rather) should begin putting thought into what defines the form it takes. I can't help but wonder *who* this Branchwalker used to be prior to their transformation, though, I suppose finding that answer will be hard to come by." 

> "As for naming, I believe the name "Baldur" is best fitting. It is by far, one of the stronger and *friendliest* out of the Nordic gods, so it makes sense." 

> "As for the *crying* mentioned earlier, however, I have no current theories on what it might be. It would, in future, be a good idea to begin looking into this."
