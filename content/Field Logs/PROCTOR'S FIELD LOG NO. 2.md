**"Crystal Valley Lake Monster"**

**Location:** 
> The Docks

**Witness(es):** 
> PROCTOR, Unknown number of campers and several counselors

**Phenomenon:** 
> Creature

**What Happened:**
> A six legged, aquatic reptilian creature with glowing yellow eyes emerged from the lake after several campers caught a large catfish. This ~~godless abomination~~ creature was longer than the boats at the dock and is around two campers or so in height (due to its head and neck constantly being upright).
> After approaching the mass of campers multiple times and even coming onto shore, something occurred (most likely unwanted camper interference) which caused it to roar, which was rather load, before attacking and attempting to take away Eric. However, thanks to an effort by campers, the creature was repelled and Eric was rescued. The creature retreated to the water.

**Reactions:**
> General amazement, curiosity, slight fear from campers. Caution from the Counselors. The bravery of some campers to attack it and rescue Eric is to be commended, even if advised against. I sure as hell would've stayed away.
> The creature itself seemed rather curious to the campers, very willing to approached them before it went aggressive, on multiple occasions.

**Theory or Notes:**
> I have reason to suspect that the reason it appeared was due to the high presence of fish in that part of the lake, along with the blood that had been leaking from the larger catfish caught by campers.
> How it was so willing to approach the campers and staff was rather strange. Either it did not feel threatened by the campers, it was too curious not to, or it might've had interactions with campers in the past.
> Raelan believed it might've been either of paranatural origin or of some sort of mutated origin, possibly a mutated gator though, I think its some sort of undiscovered, natural creature.
> Regardless, I am no longer swimming in that part of the lake. At least not without supervision.
