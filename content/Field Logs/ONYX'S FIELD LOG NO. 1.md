"**Black anomaly.**"

Time: 2:45-3:15 am cst
Location: Cabin to the right of boathouse. 
Witness(es): Onyx, Ruby, 3 other camper, Siren

Phenomenon: Visual, sound, touch..** regeneration?**

What Happened:
Heavy rain and hale began, follow by loud thunder and lightning. a dark figure stood outside the cabin window, though, when called out, moved out of sight. Moments Later, it showed up behind me, however I did not see it this time. Though, once again, when called out, it moved out of sight. Shortly after, I heard an intense ticking noise. Like that of a clock. it was quiet at first, but grew loud, overwhelmingly so. It made my head throb. Shortly after that, my missing arm began to hurt. Unlike a pain I've had before, other than when i lost it. I could feel something growing. I had the insatiable desire to scratch, to itch, **and then from the formerly healed skin, a black extremity began to grow.** **The ticking still drowned out every noise- every word my friends spoke. I could not listen. I could not think.  When it was done growing, it was half the size of a normal arm, and withered. blackened.** I can *feel*  it, and move it, just slightly. 

Conditions:
 Surrounding the moment, the weather darkened, and heavy rain fell along with hale, despite the temperatures not being fitting. Thunder and lightning shook the cabin. As soon as the moment ended, the weather cleared. 

Reactions:
Others in the room could not hear what I did. It did not show itself to me. But it surrounded me with an overwhelming ticking. a relentless ticking. It hurt my /head/. At first, the pain in my arm, the things i saw portruding only I saw. Just a black.. thing. **But as the arm grew, the others in the room could see.  I was horrified. terrified. I could not think anything besides fear. **

Theory or Notes: 
If this is connected to the "tetherman", it comes after imogen's followers and those associated. Imogen had always promised me a new arm. I may have been naive, but now I have this sad excuse for a new one. It came for me finally. Or, of course, it's due to Indie and I breaking off that piece of the dead pillar at the stonehenge. I will try to train the strength of this new hand, and see if it can become semi-functional. at the moment, I can hardly move it besides small twitches, and I have full feeling. I can still hear the ticking now. it hasn't stopped, but it's not as overwhelming.
