***"MISTAKES"***
-# "All the progress I've made -- It's gone. There's nowhere further to go."

__**CONDITIONS**__
> **TIME:** Around midnight. I want to say close to 11pm or 12am.
> **LOCATION:** Stonehenge 1, later travelled down towards Group A to evacuate. 

__**ANOMALIES, ABNORMALITIES, ENTITIES OR OTHER**__
> - Bushman appearance.
> - Mothlight.

## RUN DOWN
__**STONEHENGE VISIT**__
> "I followed Aida down to the Henges. She was saying something about wanting to visit them - despite my warnings about how late it had turned. She was... adamant - so to make sure she *wouldn't* get herself killed, I came along."

> "It was quiet, at first. Aida retrieved a stick of chalk from her bag and went to try and draw on one of the henges. I warned her - in time, luckily. She didn't even lean a single line on the pillar. I had to raise my voice to really get the word across, but she gave up after a while. Though, thinking about it, maybe raising my voice was what got us into this mess."

> "Bushman appeared from within the circle , blitzing past the pillars to rush immediately into my face. He didn't attack - no - he just stood there, ***staring*** at me. If it were anyone else, he might have killed them - especially after the results of last night. But no. He just... looked at me. **[1]**"

> "He told me to leave. And to never return to the site again." 

__**LEAVING**__
> "Unwounded, Aida and I left the site, hurriedly going to make our way back to camp. All seemed peaceful, until something came crawling out from the ground." 

__**MOTHLIGHT**__
> "I don't know how to explain it - but I really do mean it when I say he returned. This time as a spirit, though. I don't think the *living* Mothlight was able to just phase through the ground like that **[2]**. He spoke to us -- ***clearly***. I've attached a transcript of what he said." 

> __**START OF DIALOGUE**__
> "Don't you want to know what's on the other side?"
> "Don't you miss your friends?"
> "I heard all of you speaking earlier." 
> "You seem... lonely. Like you miss them."
> "It's fine. I can't hurt you." ... "...Even if I wanted to."
> "It's beyond the Shade."
> "You're a small fish in the ocean." 
> "...Trying to know what flying in the sky's like." 
> "Find a trench. Hide in it." 
> "...Or do yourself a favor, and jump into the mouth of the nearest shark." 
> "Your mind's better off mercifully silenced." 

> "I didn't even get time to ask him a question. He disappeared soon after. Aida and myself made haste to leave, aswell. It wasn't long before the fog rolled in again. **[3]**"

__**EVACUATING**__
> "On our way back, we ran into some girl with black hair. Just seemed like a camper. I told her to make her way back indoors and passed her a Dreamcatcher I had been carrying at the time. She seemed to be trying to find her own way back, herself. Erving swung by, too, just to relay the same message I had. Whatever it was, knowledge of Mothlight's appearance had already become known across the Counselors."

> "Wasn't long before I saw Mothlight again - just walking across my vision in the fog. **[4]**" 

> "We carried on, making our way along and all the way towards the Boathouse. I tried to knock on Indie's cabin, but was met with no response. Aida, who I had left for *just* a moment, had frozen up. **[5]** I tried talking to her - tried snapping her out of whatever trance she was at the time, but got no response until she found her way out naturally." 

> "Once that was over, we made our way back to our cabins and hunkered down. There was nothing more, afterwards."

__**NOTES AND THEORIES**__
> "I don't know what to say." 

> "Mothlight is dead. There shouldn't be a doubt about it. He couldn't hurt us, he said that himself. He's simply back as a spirit to... *warn* us about what's going on. ~~Warn. What a shitty way of putting it.~~

> "I don't know what to make of his whole monologue. Still trying to figure out what he meant by "It's beyond the Shade.". Perhaps there's something we're missing regarding the Gates? Regardless, finding out is a poor option. Ignorance is a mercy."

> "I likely cant return to the gates. I think, if I were to *one* more time, Bushman would have me killed for it. I'm pushing my luck too far -- probably have by now. This expedition has only backtracked my progress. Do what you will with what I saw. I need to go and get some rest."
