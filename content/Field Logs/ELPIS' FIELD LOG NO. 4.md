***Anomalies seen***

***Context***
> "Me and CESARE stumbled upon the scout's camping site, and almost immediately, we saw Jormungandr approaching campers. 
> 
> What he did was unlike what he had done before, however"

***Rot***
> "At first, with the first person he grabbed, people managed to keep a bit of distance. Fortunately, seemingly because of this, he let her go. There was maybe . . . . Ten minutes of peace before he showed himself again, to which he preceded to grab yet ANOTHER camper, and this time he did not let them go. He hauled them over to the lake, and seemingly held her hostage. Anytime people would try and grab the camper or otherwise try and oppose him, he'd back further into the lake. Once we managed to get the crowd of campers to back away, only then did he let her go, and vanish"

***Aftermath***
> "As much as the branchwalker itself went, it appeared to be the same. It was large, still dark, and had all the same growths and the same long neck. However, what i did notice were the injuries it left on the neck of the poor girl it had grabbed. Instead of your usual bruising and maybe the occasional gash, the flesh on her neck was rotted, almost like whatever touched her maybe had aged her or simply just caused the skin on her neck to decay. As far as i know, she was brought to the infirmary"

***Thoughts***
