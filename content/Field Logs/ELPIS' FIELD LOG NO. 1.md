***Setting***
> "The dead walk the earth"
> - Time: Late night
> - Local: Near Hart House

***Anomalies seen***
> - The Undead, possibly just spirits

***Context***:
> "The event was a relatively abnormal one to begin with. A good chunk of campers gathered near Hart House for some sort of celebration, at the time I did now know what, but upon arrival I noticed the large array of food. 
> 
> This would be odd if it was literally ANYONE other than the Girns hosting this. The event was put in place to celebrate them in fact, and for a good while, the event went on normally. That was until someone shouted "Zombies" "

***The Undead***
> "From the edge of the beach came shouts and yells. Two campers came running in yelling about zombies, and for a moment I just thought them to be crazy, that was until the dead they were talking about actually came out behind them. They seemed to be Pilgrims, or at least dressed like them, and with them some carried weaponry. One had an axe, another a cleaver, and one even had a working musket, but that's besides the point. They took over the stage, and gave us the option to either let them "Retake" their land, or . . . Well the details are fuzzy but it's suffice to say no one gave them opposition in the beginning. Though, rather quickly things started to go downhill, people with dyed hair or frankly just people at random began to be shunned as witches. From that moment on, things only got more and more out of hand, starting with the "Mayor" getting attacked, ending with him being killed. That initiated somewhat of a . . . Revolution"

***Uprising***
> "With the "Mayor" dead, the other male undead began to try and  make their way to the stage, though they were met with opposition. People began to arm themselves, and in return, the undead began to be more forceful. They threatened and push and such until they all gathered on the stage, but almost as soon as they were all in one place, people began to rush the stage. The undead fought back of course, injury a multitude of people, but the collective effort of the campers started to slowly start to bring down the undead. As for what happened at the stage for the rest of the night, I don't actually know, I got . . . preoccupied. What happened specifically on my side of things isnt important, but me and two other campers did our part in dealing with the threat of the undead. The night ended with everyone being forced back to their cabins by who I'm assuming was Greene Girn himself"

***Aftermath and Afterthoughts***
> "Luckily for everyone, there were no casualties, only injuries, some more severe than other, but still, no deaths. As far as theories go, I've heard a few things. Some said that they were simply spirits, but I for one beg to differ, the spirits that we know of are immaterial, untouchable, and due to that, unkillable. Obviously what's dead cannot be killed, but with the Pilgrims, that idea is flipped on its head. They were physical, they could interact with the people around them, and they most definitely weren't from our time. As far as how or what made these things come back to life, I have no clue, but I think calling them spirits would be wrong, the sole fact that they could be "Killed" means that they were much more than ghosts. Which leaves us with a big question and an even bigger problem, what the fuck made these things come back? . . . Food for thought, I guess"
