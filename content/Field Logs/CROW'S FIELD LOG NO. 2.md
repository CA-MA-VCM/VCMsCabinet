## **The Damned and The Savior**
> 
> "It began later into the evening when the fog began to set in. The group of us shared worries that it could lead to another **Amberman** attack, so we holed up inside of the **Shed** outside of the **Lighthouse**. After waiting in there for a good amount of time, the cold began to set in so, I suggested that we move further down towards the **Lake House**."
> 
> "Upon our arrival, it was all normal. Until, outside I saw **Kiana** making her way up the dock towards us, so I let her in. She made a comment about the smell of ash being in the air, which I learned is something that only ghosts can really smell. –– This is around the time I noticed the radio static's sound beginning to get louder. –– After speaking with her for a while, and none of the normal signs of **Amberman** attacking (*ie, Roaring, People Screaming, Orange Lightning*), I decided to step outside on the dock for a brief moment to look around."

> "I stepped back into the cabin to be greeted by the arrival of three other campers joining up with us."

> "When I turned back around, however, the sound of the static grew louder as the **Poltergeist** stepped closer. Not even a few minutes later, the ghost of **Trent**–better known as "**Pyre**"–appeared and began conversing with **Kiana**. He "shed" his skin and gained some bloody, acid-burned, corpse look. –– We ran up the **Lighthouse**, not the best plan I know, he told us to either jump out a window, or hide at the top. He threw a chair at **Samantha**, but couldn't physically touch it. Then he dropped a candle and set the **Lighthouse** on fire, following that by pouring some flammable alcohol onto us so it hurt more." 
> 
> "**Kiana** stepped in and fought with him. I believe if she wasn't there, we would've died. The same **Orange Lightning** that I keep seeing struck **"Pyre"** down and sent him out of the window. The fire was put out and we all escaped safely."
