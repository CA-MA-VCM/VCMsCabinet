***"MEMORIES"***
-# "Even after all these years, I still miss you; I don't think that feeling will ever pass."

__**CONDITIONS**__
> **TIME:** Late afternoon leading into the Evening.
> **LOCATION:** Church

__**ANOMALIES, ABNORMALITIES, ENTITIES OR OTHER**__
> - Branchwalker sighting

__**PREFACE**__
> "As of recent, a few of us have noticed that the Church has been slowly "repairing" itself. I've taken it on my own accord (seeing as I can't visit the Henges all that often now) to do regular check-ups on it across the past few weeks. This now marks the third check-up." 
> 
> "As for changes, there haven't been any *significant* differences from the last visit. The piano remains fixed and the candle at the alter still remains lit. Nothing else - nothing more." 
> 
> "Leaving, however, led me to a... *interesting* sight." 

__**BRANCHWALKER SIGHTING**__
> "Moments on stepping out, the wind stopped abruptly. The air had turned completely still and, as soon as a bush rustled in the distance, the birds went silent aswell. It was only, once all of those noises disappeared, did I hear the faint hum of... *something* in the distance."
> "...It's pretty-fucking-obvious what came out next." 

> "Stepping out from the bushes, one of the Branchwalkers we saw the other day stepped out. Notable features include: 
> - Long, spindly arms. 
> - A singular eye, cracked at the bottom to look almost like a teardrop. 
> - ~~Two~~ Three horns, each sprouting a flower at the top.  

> "It got... *REALLY* close, perhaps a few feet away from me. The evident "hum" that followed it was actually close to that of crying -- a child crying, to be exact." 
> 
> "But it wasn't interested in me. It was interested in something else." 
> 
> "My jacket. __Grandpa's__ jacket." 

> "I didn't give it at first. Tried to offer it something else, or *try* offering it something else, atleast. I tried the ring Aida gave me -- to which it shook it's head. I tried the lighter, which *seemed* to appease it more, but it was still focused on the jacket. I didn't want to give it away, but... fuck, anything for results."
> 
> "I gave it. The moment I did, though, it immediately started to... jump in joy? The crying shifted to something closer to that of laughter and, not a moments after, it gave me one of it's flowers *and* a hug (which hurt like a bitch, mind you.)."
> 
> "It decided to put it on and, as if recognizing the chevrons, began to march back and forth in a line like a soldier. It was playing with it; just like I used to when I was younger. It was... I don't know, nostalgic? It was like I was watching myself when I used to be a little shit."  
> 
> "...I miss those days." 
> 
> "...Back to the point at hand -- it decided to take the jacket with it, disappearing back into the forest. I left shortly after aswell." 

__**NOTES & THEORIES**__
> "Honestly, I'm... uncertain about what I just saw." 

> "First things first is that it's definitely more *child-like* compared to the other Branchwalkers, both in personality and presence. Though, I should reiterate that it wasn't... *particularly* interested in me, moreso the memoirs I carried. Hell, it was practically jumping for joy when I gave the jacket over, so theres certainly *some* kind of importance there other than it just looking 'cool'." 

> "Christ, I can't help but feel almost... regretful that I gave it away. At least I still have his lighter -- but...I'm going to miss it." 

> "We'll need to look into it further. Same with that *other* new arrival."
