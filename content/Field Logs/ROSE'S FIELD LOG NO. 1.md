## __THE HUNT.__

` 10/7/1987 - EVENING - THE SCOUT LODGE`
`WITNESSES: Evelyn, Marina, Cal, Indie, Leila`  

### Regarding last nights events in the evening.
> As instructed, me and Evelyn started our evening by attending the movie night down at the Scout Lodge. Although this was my first movie night, Evelyn informed me that the other two events ended up with the projector in flames. We had a big group of people showing up at the event. Marina had asked us to take note of the reaction of our fellow campers so that's what I will go over now. When the film started, most people seemed to be focused on the movie. I noticed some campmates talking amongst themselves, not paying attention. However, when the projector started playing up, I noticed more frustration amongst our campmates, and some of their frustration directed at the boy who stood on top of the projector.... I have no idea *why* he would do such a thing.
> 
> I witnessed a councillor grounding the child in the Scouts lodge. Other campers looked for solutions, with a small crowd looking to fix the projector. Marina decided to try and grab a television, however due to the weight of it, she dropped it and it broke. At that point, it seemed like the event had to end early.

### Activity Spike with wildlife.
> Shortly after the events just mentioned, I, among many others noticed wildlife around the Scouts Lodge. There was a few deer's and a pig. I kept an eye on people's reactions and the main thing I noticed is that most of our campers would either try to pet the animals, crowd the animals or shoo the animals away. I kept a close eye knowing that hurting the wildlife could bring upon Bushman so I knew it might be best to talk to people and make sure they didn't do anything disrespectful.
> 
> Thats when I saw our campmate Marty amongst a crowd of people surrounding a pig. The pig seemed pretty startled with the huge crowd around them. Marty had started talking about finding a knife to kill the pig, which is when I approached him to tell him to please respect the wildlife. He didn't seem to take any notice of what I was saying, and after a few moments, decided to grab the back leg of the pig and attacked it. Multiple people called him out for his behaviour, including Arnold. This lead Marty to lash out at him, headbutting him. I'd never seen someone get so violent so quickly. I left the scene shortly after, seeing that my warnings were going unheard and thought it best to not give him the attention.
> 
> I then continued watching the wildlife, doing my best not to scare them. I was surprised that they got so close to the campers, as there were big crowds, however due to my lack of time at Camp Astrid, I'm not sure if this is.... *normal*

### Bushman Hunt
> A little while later in the evening, I noticed that to the side of the Scout Lodge, Marty was talking to a few people. I thought it best to be a bit nosy and eavesdrop the conversation. Thats when I heard his intentions to go out to hunt the Bushman, with Amy trying to persuade him otherwise. This little group started gaining a crowd, with many new campers wanting to follow him into the woods. Some to help hunt the Bushman, others to prove of his existence. 
> 
> Now it might have been quite foolish, but I went alongside Evelyn to see if we could talk some people out of going into the woods. I thought that if multiple people tried to warn them, it might be enough to make them realise this was dangerous. None of them listened. I saw multiple campers split off alone into the wood or try to provoke the Bushman.
> 
> ... But in the end they got exactly what they wanted when the Bushman showed up. A crowd watched as he attacked campers, and many people didn't listen. Me and Eve tried to tell people to run and leave but again, it was all ignored. With the situation escalating. Marina, Evelyn and I decided to evacuate to the Lighthouse, no longer wishing to put our lives in danger.

### Running to the Lighthouse
> I have the feeling while we fled to the Lighthouse that we weren't alone. It was a bit of a treck to get there but the three of us made it. Marina asked us to grab shovels, which still I'm unsure what we might have done with them, but we went up to the top of the lighthouse. Marina decided it be best to go back to find our other members of ARC so me and Evelyn stayed at the top of the Lighthouse. We stayed there until it was over.

### Summary.
> It was an evening to remember... I had... never seen violence to that level before... and I'd never seen that much blood before. However it has also taught me something, that most people will not listen to people's warnings. All I can do is attempt to reason with them, but I think now that after I try 3 times and it doesn't seem to be working, I will leave them to their choices. I don't want to get hurt. I don't want Evelyn or Kacy to get hurt either, so I will be brave enough to speak up and warn people, but not reckless to stay in the holes they dig for themselves.
