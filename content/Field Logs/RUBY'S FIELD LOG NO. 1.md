# THE STRANGE SHADOW
*"I'm never gettin' mad at anyone ever again."*

## CONDITIONS
> * **Time**: 03:00 AM - 04:00 AM EST
> * **Location**: Inside one of the cabins at Group B, located to the right of the boathouse.
> * **Weather**: Rain, thunder, hail.
> * **Witnesses**: `[RUBY]`, `[ONYX]`, `[FENRIR]`, `[SIREN]`, and three other campers.

## PHENOMENA
> * VISUAL SIGHTINGS OF ANOMALY.
> * INVOKED FEELINGS OF DREAD.
> * LIMB MANIPULATION.

## WHAT HAPPENED
> ### PREFACE
> "It started after I finished yelling at everybody. It was such a stupid situation, but I was upset, and I was going in on everybody there, including `[ONYX]`, who I may have chewed out the most. While we were in the process of recovering from that tense, but resolved, confrontation, the weather changed. I heard the soft murmurs of rain before it exploded into thunder. The thunder was persistent, but then, the hail started. We heard loud thuds on the rooftop, so we glanced out the windows to see balls of ice falling onto the ground. That's when we decided no one would leave the cabin under any circumstances."
> 
> ### THE SIGHTINGS
> "When I turned around, that's when I saw it. It was a humanoid figure, but it was all black. The anomaly was not transparent by any means, and I don't recall ever seeing a face, but I do remember the feeling of being watched and feeling dread before I even spotted it. I ended up seeing it twice. The first time, it lingered outside of the cabin's living room window located on its right wall. By the time a couple of us had pointed it out, it shuffled to the right. While I attempted to cheer everyone up in hopes it would make the entity go away, it appeared in the window behind `[ONYX]`. This window was the one located on the left side of the cabin's doors. That was the second time I saw it. It stepped out of sight like the first time when a couple of us pointed it out. We covered up the windows with blankets after that."
> 
> ### THE HALLUCINATIONS
> "Right after the second sighting, `[ONYX]` asked if we heard something. I asked what he meant, and he asked if we heard ticking. We said no. He continued to insist that there was no way we weren't hearing that ticking noise he was hearing. He described it like a clock. He then got up from the couch and stood in front of the window located on the cabin's left wall. I stood in front of him, never leaving his side as his hallucinations continued to worsen. `[ONYX]` can describe what he was experiencing better than I can, but he was panicked. I told him to breathe with me, and it seemed to help a bit, but then his face contorted into pain, and at first he tried to brush it off, but the pain got worse. He started scratching at his bandages, saying something was growing, but I continued to tell him that it was all part of his hallucinations."
> 
> ### THE WITHERED ARM
> "Something was in fact growing. `[ONYX]` was right. From where his bandage was, a small, black arm began to grow. The hand was the size of a child's, and it stopped growing at the point where `[ONYX]`'s elbow would normally be at. I stepped back out of shock, and `[ONYX]` begged me not to leave him. I immediately returned to his side, watching as he grabbed this new arm. Nothing happened when he grabbed it. It was around this time that `[FENRIR]` and `[SIREN]` knocked on the door, and my friend let them in. I caught `[FENRIR]` and `[SIREN]` up on the situation as best as I could in the middle of this chaotic moment. I told `[ONYX]` to let `[FENRIR]` check up on him, and I pulled `[SIREN]` to the bedroom to tell her about what `[ONYX]` had told me (see **ADDITIONAL NOTES & THEORIES** for more information)." 

> ### AFTERMATH
> "`[SIREN]` and I returned to the living room where `[FENRIR]` asked `[ONYX]` if he knew about black wax. `[ONYX]` said he did, but that he stays far away from it. `[ONYX]` spoke about the cult, the Tetherman, and Imogen's promise to him, attempting to find a reason for why all of this might have happened. `[FENRIR]` also asked `[ONYX]` if he could move his new, withered arm, but it seemed like it could only twitch. At least, that was the case at first. After a bit, `[ONYX]` was able to move it a bit, albeit to an extremely limited degree. Once `[FENRIR]` finished his check-up on `[ONYX]`, he told `[SIREN]` and I to meet him outside. A few seconds after we stepped out of the cabin, the rain stopped almost as quickly as it came. It was also there that I also told `[FENRIR]` about what `[ONYX]` had told me while he was experiencing his hallucinations."

## ADDITIONAL NOTES & THEORIES
> "During `[ONYX]`'s hallucinations, he told me that the reason all of this might be happening is because he and `[POLLUX]` broke off a piece from the dead Stonehenge pillar located in the meadows. I informed `[FENRIR]` and `[SIREN]` about this to gather their thoughts on this theory, and we all agreed that it could be correlated. However, it is important to note down that Imogen's promise to `[ONYX]` was to give him a new arm, and The Entity was directly tied to Imogen."
> 
> "Considering this is the second time I have encountered an all black humanoid figure during a moment where I was severely upset, I am on edge. The first time, though, it targeted me. I felt growing dread, fear, and unease, but nobody saw what I did. However, I think that while similar, these two anomalies are different from one another. At least, they looked different from one another. I remember when I encountered one of these for the first time, it was grinning. Regardless, members should be on alert when the weather suddenly changes, for this instance definitely seemed to be directly linked to a drastic weather change."
> 
> "Stay safe out there." 
> 
> — ***RUBY***
