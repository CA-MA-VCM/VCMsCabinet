*DATE*: 11/02/87
*TIME*: ~2 PM
*LOCATION*: Lighthouse
*WITNESSES*: Lyric, a crowd of campers.

__*PHENOMENON*__
- Ghost Sighting (Kiana)
- A chill in the air
- Accidental Mindreading (?)
- Amber Man sighting

__*CONDITIONS*__
- Blue light at the lighthouse

__*EVENTS*__
> Kiana had showed up at the foot of the lighthouse. Her mood was sour, and she claimed she could "hear it." "The souls passing out of the land of the living."
> I think the deaths yesterday brought her out here. However, it was at this point I thought to myself 'Why was she here?', and she responded as if I said it out loud.
> Kiana wasn't looking at me when this happened, and I didn't even realize until a few seconds later that __that__ just happened.
> The crowd grew, and she said she couldn't tell who's saying what anymore. I tried to test if she could hear my thoughts a few more times, but no response.
> Below is a transcript, starting from when she responded to my thoughts.
> 
> "I'm here, because ... Why was I here again?"
> 
> "Sorry, I thought there was a concert. I was gonna be late?"
> 
> "How many concerts?"
> 
> "I can't tell who's saying what anymore."
> 
> (At this point she was struggling to respond to anyone, but someone asked how Marina is doing.)
> 
> "Don't know ... I don't know if she knows herself."
> 
> "I wanted to meet her too. See if she was like Maria."
> 
> It was at this point she seemed to become overwhelmed from the crowd, as she crouched down and covered her ears.
> I can't blame her. One guy started asking about 'The Goonies'?
> She claimed she was going to be late to something, and that 'Chika' would be upset. She also named Dia, Mari, and 'the eight of them.'
> 
> Unfortunately, the investigation was derailed at this point by a blonde guy who claimed to "know her", and that he "knew what to do" here. All he did was agitate Kiana, making her escape into the lighthouse.
> A counselor also did not let us into the building either. A few minutes later, everyone was forced to evacuate as the amber man attacked.
> Lyric and I took shelter in the lighthouse after a short panic. Unfortunately, that blonde guy had also gone in and agitated Kiana further. She yelled at everyone to leave.
> What the counselor and Kiana spoke about (if at all?) I don't know.

__*REACTIONS*__
> You read it earlier in the report. People crowded her.
> Can we actually like, __do__ something about this? Are we allowed to?
> I don't want to upset people by trying to push them away, but you don't need to ask a ghost about The Goonies. What even is that?
> Just some weird movie from two years ago? I think I saw it in a blockbuster once.

__*THEORIES AND NOTES*__
> I'm unsure. Kiana seems to be falling apart mentally, but I don't know why she could hear my thoughts.
> I think she only responded to mine, but only once. Can ghosts just do that?
> Maybe she responded to others and I just didn't know. I can't read minds, how would I know? She did talk about everyone being too loud. Maybe she could hear everyone's thoughts?
> No solid theories. Just confusion.
