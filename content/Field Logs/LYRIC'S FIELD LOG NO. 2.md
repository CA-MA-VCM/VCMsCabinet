Date: ***10/14/87***
Time: ***Around 3-4 PM***
Location: ***Beach***
Witnesses: PARIAH, ROSE

Phenomenon: ***Ghost Sighting***

Details:
*"Around the middle of the day, an apparition, later identified as "Angela", walked out from the treeline and onto the beach. Upon noticing her, I, along with Lisa and a few others, approached and waited to hear what she was there for. This, of course, is because of the spirits rather volatile temper, but that's not the point of this. Angela appeared to study the campers for a moment before announcing herself outright, similarly to how she did at the gas station"*

Conversation:

*"The first person she singled out to speak to was me, and immidietly prompted me to explain the incidents that occured during the rodeo. I of course, not wanting to disrespect, explained what happened to the best of my abilities. Upon hearing about the "Amber Man", she asked me to elaborate further on him, and just like before, I did so to keep her present for as long as possible."*

"Disrespect":

*"After hearing my explanation of events, she dissipated for a moment after hearing someone, not exactly sure who, make a comment about her. She reappeared around said person and began to screech about not interrupting. Upon asking if out parents ever taught us manners, I blurted out that not everyone came from the same place. and thus might've not been taught the same things as others. This was. . . . . My mistake really, but still, her attention was turned back onto me. She began to ask me why people didn't teach each other. I answered that we didn't cause we didn't all know each other, and she asked the simple question of why.  Of course, I couldn't answer that so for the duration of the appearance, I decided to keep my mouth shut"*

The continued warning of time:

*"For a large part of her conversation's with people. a sort of pattern started to show. The pattern being her expressing her opinion of people who either A, made a comment about her, or B, outright spoke out against her. Now, by itself, this doesn't mean much, but it's what these conversations lead to that might allude to something. She brought up the issue of limited time again, same thing she talked about at the gas station. She said that we should cherish out time since we're running out of it, and apparently, it didn't sit right with other campers, me included. One theory that I heard is that she knows something more, but that's just it, a theory"*

Conclusion: 

*"Angela has shown herself to be a rather. . . . . Moody? I guess that could be the right word for it- But either way, she's prone to bouts of rage. Other than that however, we really haven't been able to get any notable information out of her. Still, it's useful to try and get to understand these spirits. Lis has more info if need be"*
