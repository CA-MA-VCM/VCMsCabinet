## Conditions & Other
- *TIME:* Late evening
- *LOCATION:* Group A (Cafeteria)
- *WEATHER:* Rain
- *WITNESSES:* CESARE, Clyde, Jean, Mich (Maintenance)

## Phenomena
- Spirit(?) sighting
- Whispers
- Telepathic communication
- Auditory hallucinations
- Temporary blindness
- Mimicry of our late 'LYRIC'

## Preface
> For a lot of this, other people were with me. They could see it. I know they could. But even so, I don't know how much of this was real. I felt insane the entire way through, especially when I heard things the others couldn't. But I know they could see it. I think.

## Phase One — Stalking
> A dark silhouette kept appearing in places and angles only I could see, around the cafeteria. Most of the time it would stare at me, and disappear right as I pointed it out to someone else. I felt like I was going insane.

> At one point during this 'phase', it began to get into my head. I felt watched. I couldn't see it, but it could see me, and it made sure I knew. I eventually found it staring at me, and it disappeared behind the side of the cafeteria building. Not long after I saw it go into the cafeteria itself, so I chased it to the balcony.
> Again, I was the only person who saw any of this, and Mich followed me to the balcony. At this point he asked me if I'm okay in the head, have mental issues etc. and he accompanied me back to the porch.

> At this point, I thought I'd gone crazy. Like, actually just fucking nuts. When it showed up again, I refused to acknowledge it. But this time, the others saw it. Mich went up to it, but it disappeared before he actually got close. We hid in the cafeteria, and this is when it grew bolder.

## Phase Two — Mimicry
> It knocked at one of the windows, before there was this whispering. I don't think anyone else made out what it said, but I did. "Come here, Kacy. It hurts. Come out here. Please."
> Outside, we found it again. But it looked like LYRIC. Like, exactly like her. The only difference was that it still looked like a shadow, like pitch black. And she didn't have a face.
> She walked at me, and disappeared after phasing through me. At this point it got worse, and began putting sounds into my head, and speaking in her voice. Just without the accent. She yelled about how 'it hurts', which was cut short with the sound of wood creaking and a final crunch. And then she told me to come to the cabin alone. 'It hurts.'

> I know I should not have gone alone. But I did. I told CESARE that I wanted to be alone for a bit. Going into the cabin, I just barely fucking saw her walking into the bedroom. After following and calling out to her, she kept speaking behind me. Asked me why I didn't save her. How all I had to do was help her. Yelling about how it was all my fault. Every time I turned to face her she disappeared.

> She finally appeared in the corner of the room, and walked at me. I couldn't see anything anymore, and I heard the crack of LYRIC's skull again. I wasn't sure if I *wanted* to be safe. I'm not entirely sure that thought was, like, mine. Really mine. But the guilt was just horrible.
> I could see again and just collapsed. The rain cleared up shortly after. I don't know if that's related.

## Notes & Theories
> I'm not sure this is even a spirit/ghost. It didn't have that blue glow, or anything normal for spirits. It couldn't have been LYRIC. Not actually. I think. And other people did see part of it, so I'm not hallucinating.
> Whatever it is, I think it's either got some way to 'access' the spirits of dead people, or to access your memories. Maybe it's similar, or related, to Mothlight? As to why it decided to do all of this, I have no fucking clue. We know fear makes people more susceptible to the paranormal. Maybe it's that, or a similar version but for other negative emotions?
