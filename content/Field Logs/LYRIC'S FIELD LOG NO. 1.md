***The Woods***
      10/11/1987 - Nighttime - the forest, bridges, and the rodeo
Witnesses: Too many to count

***The landslide***
"Things are a little fucked:
Yesterday was probably one of the worst incidents as of recent, only because of the rippled it had throughout the camp. Ill get to explaining the incident though. At about 11-12 PM, directly at the rodeo, the sky turned a strange shade of blue, which was accompanied by a deluge of stones from the nearby valleys. This caused the crowds within the Rodeo to flee into the neighboring woods to get out of harm way. What followed can only be described as a seismic event, rock slide, and when we came back to the rodeo, the whole place was subsequently destroyed. This in and of itself was a disaster, but what came after was much, much worse. 

***The hillbillies***:
After everyone got their bearings, me, Kacy, Lisa, and Simon included, everyone quickly caught wind of three individuals watching over the distraught crowd up on a cliff. The three did not openly speak to us, but they did seem to almost study us for a moment before backing off. This was followed by multiple spottings of Branchwalkers by myself and my compatriots, Marina included, and a couple more members of ARC. Marina gave me the instructions to gather the member, but unfortunately before I could get anyone together, people started to move. I tried my best to stick with the group, but unfortunately the group would get split as well. 

***The altercation***:
The group was unable to cross the bridge into the main camp due to the presence of a multitude of Branchwalkers and the presence of the bushman. Unbeknownst to us at the time as well, the Amber Man was nearby chasing after the group. Me and Kacy both witnessed the Bushman and accompanying Branchwalkers attack the Amber before we both fled to get to safety. Though, at the time, we did so without Lisa or any of the other ARC members, and I was split off from Kacy.

***Refuge***:
After witnessing the death of a camper from a far, I managed to find my way to the secondary bridge leading into the main camp, and followed s small group until eventually I met back up with Kacy, Calliope, and Marina, where then we traversed all the way back up to the cafeteria to regroup. We took refuge afterwards in a cabin below, staying there until other campers came to tell us the coast was clear. 

Summary:
Im trying my best to sound professional here, but thing are bad. Really bad, I mean we don't know how many are even dead yet- So far I've only heard of one but I don't doubt people are injured. ARC could've been more organized here, it seemed we kept getting split up and caught up in our agendas that we forgot the golden rule, but I digress, we were lucky to make it out of there alive. Not everything bad though, if anything we've learned something, the Bushman and the Amber man aren't at all affiliated, maybe we could use that to our advantage?"
