***Setting***
> "Fog and Walking, lots of walking"
> - Time: Late night - Morning
> - Local: Near the lake and woodland area

***Anomalies seen***
> - Possibly a new entity all together
> - Branchwalker: Odin

***Context***: 
> "This was not any kind of special hosted event, or even a very serious one in general, but the things seen match up with other reports of similar phenomenon, and because of such, I feel this is important to log. 
> 
> The sole reason for this expedition was a rather thick fog, more thick than before, that and odd noises heard throughout the night"

***First investigation***
> "What happened before I met with Miss Chambers is irrelevant, though upon coming across each other, we both agreed the fog was a bad omen, and thus needed to be monitored. As we walked however, the first thing that truly clued us in that something more might've been going on, was the distant sounds of something whimpering near the lake. Upon hearing this, Miss Chambers and I headed off to investigate. Now, at first, it seemed as if maybe things were actually non anomalous for once, just a weird fog and maybe an injured animal. Whether or not it was anything out of the ordinary more or less remains to be seen, but further into our investigation, Miss Chambers redirected us to some sort of ritual circle, upon which she read a book yadda yadda. That also led to nothing, and upon which, me and her decided to throw in the towel"

***Post Fog***
> "As me and Miss Chambers were on our way back to camp however, we were stopped by Odin. We don't know how long he was watching us, but seemingly he had seen us at the ritual circle, and either wanted to just simply let us know he saw, he warn us away from it. Either way, for some reason, he gave us the skull that was at the site. Why this is? I have absolutely no clue, but we took it upon ourselves after to deliver it back to the site. Nothing note worthy happened, other than a continuous feeling i had whilst traveling. It's not a rare feeling out in the woods, I felt as if i was being watched. Though, looking at my observer, I was not met with a Branchwalker or spirit, but instead, a figure shrouded in shadow. I didnt get to look at him very long, but I could see the glint of what seemed to be glasses on his face"

***Afterthoughts***
> "As much as seeing Odin did intrigue me, I really dont think that should be the focal point of this report. The shadow person however, that's where i think we should focus. According to others, there's more of them, though, I didn't quite get the description of the other one, so who really knows. What I do know is that, most likely, these things aren't ghosts. Ghosts have a set appearence, usually human features, but these? These only look human in shape. Not only that, but they seem to affect people in wildly different ways. One seemed to have cured someone of a medical issue, and the one I encountered made me . . . Scared, even though mentally, I didnt feel as such"
