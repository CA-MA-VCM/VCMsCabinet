**Date:** [11/24/87]

**Time:** Evening-night

**Location:** The swamp

**Witness(es):** █████████ , ███████ and a LOT more people.

### Phenomenon: 
Whole lotta branchwalkers... and strange lights in the sky.

### What Happened:
Me, ███████, and the rest of our crew decided to explore the swamp during a stakeout, until we eventually came across a VERY tall creature covered in moss with branches sprouting out of it. It had bright white eyes and it appeared to be injured, by the way it kept roaring out in pain. Despite this, it was highly hostile-- attacking anybody who got too close or tried to provoke it. It also showed stealthy qualities, given it kept sneaking up on unsuspecting campers. It injured one camper gravely, and eventually disappeared as night turned to day.

On the next day, around the same time (evening) a strange deer creature approached our base. It was standing on its hind legs, and kept letting out this loud, weird roar. Whether it was a tactic to ward campers off or not, it didn't seem to follow through with attacking or doing anything remotely dangerous. It spent most of its time ambling around, not exactly making contact with campers but getting close enough to pose a threat. It eventually left.

Soon after, a bright light shot up from some nearby stonehenge, illuminating the entire night sky and making many heads turn. I took a few snips and decided to run forward to get a better angle, before I heard a strange sound nearby. In an act of impulsivity I ran forward to go and investigate it, abandoning my fellow campers. When I reached the source of the noise, I spotted a strange figure in the distance. A strange, almost eerie droning sound was emitting from its very being, and it was wearing something that appeared to be a mask of split emotions. It was covered in a hood and robes, and it had barbed wire wrapped around its forearm, and a huge weapon hanging from over their shoulder.

It also had a band around the same arm with what appeared to be teeth strung from it? It was hard to tell due to the thick, dense fog. This figure was also incredibly fast-- it had managed to run up the hill I was standing on before I could even blink! I was scared, truly. Besides me and that figure being there, I had also encountered a fellow camper. She appeared to be in distress, and upset with something. Before I knew it, this strange figure approached her and commanded her to jump off the cliff of the hill we were standing on, telling her to "spare us the trouble"... whoever 'us' was. The camper, surprisingly, held her ground until it eventually disappeared. After that, I left and ran back to safety. This concludes what happened.

### Conditions:
The weather was pretty normal, except for a thick fog covering the area. 

### Reactions:
I tried to keep my head on and checked on my crew as much as possible before we eventually parted ways. Everybody else shared different reactions, some shocked, some scared, and some indifferent.

### Theory or Notes:
I have noticed all of these creatures and entities came from the woods, deep inside the swamp. Perhaps they use the dense fog there to their advantage, to catch campers off guard to possibly harm them or observe them? And I believe the strange light meant something grand.. important, I'm just not sure what, yet.
