# ELPIS - 1987 - LOGBOOK:

***Setting***

> "Id say we cut it a bit close"
- Time: Late night 
- Local: Near "Heisenguard"

***Anomalies seen***
> Branchwalkers: Freyr, and a newly discovered "Idunn"

***Context***:
> "Me and Miss Chambers, as is our duty, set off  to the more remote parts of camp as to get any kind of information on where this cult may be meeting up. 

Fortunately, we found her, though not at all where we thought, and not at all in the circumstances we wanted"

***Branchwalkers***
> "It was immediate when we saw it, three branchwalkers, Freyr, Jorumgandr, and a brand new branchwalker, which I have dubbed Idunn for this log. In their company was Imogen herself, why she was with them? Unknown currently, but I assume it was another warning from the forest for her to cease her activities. Strangely enough, Freyr himself told us not to follow, and while it was eating at us to do exactly that, we instead opted to stay behind and control the small crowd that had formed at the area we were at. Luckily, they actually listened for once, so for a bit at least we were able to keep the area clear. Of course that did not last long"

***Missing persons***
> "It wasn't until we started to hear shrieking coming from the forest that we truly decided to head in to find Imogen, and if it wasn't for the others following, we might've gone deeper into the forest at first, but they did exactly that. We told them to go back, and it wasn't until the branchwalkers themselves came and confronted us that they did so. Once we confirmed the others were at a safe distance, we followed the screams until we found her. Imogen, on the floor, a broken leg, and a friend at her side. How she got such an injury is up in the air, but I assume one of the branchwalkers gave it to her. Even still, we helped her, gave her a makeshift cast, and a stick for a crutch, and told her friend to take her to the infirmary. Afterwards, me and Miss Chambers checked out for the night"

***Afterthoughts***
> "The real focus here is the new branchwalker. I'll leave a quick description here since I don't really have much to say on it, it was a darkish color, horns similar to most of the others, but with distinct fruits growing on it. As far as abilities go, I believe it's able to produce these Fruits at will, with it doing so and giving me one at the time of this investigation. The fruits are normal as far as I know. All of this is just to say, I hope, for the love of GOD, and Imogen might start coming back to her sense after this"
