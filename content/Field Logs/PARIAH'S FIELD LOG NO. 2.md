*DATE*: 10/17/87
*TIME*: 1:30am - 3:30am
*LOCATION*: Hart House, Birch Forest, Swamp
*WITNESS(ES)*: Calliope Chambers, *Vivianne and the Socialites*

*__PHENOMENON(S)__* 
- Branchwalker sighting(s)

__*PREFACE*__

> "Before this Log is even considered, I need to clear a few things." 

> "This information, for now, does not leave ARC grounds. I'm aware we already have a rule set in place to gate-keep information, but I've dealt with *more* than enough investigators in the past who ramble every finding they get to complete randoms. For now, keep this information to yourself. Don't say anything. I need to keep my chances as high as possible." 

> "Two: reading this, one might come to assume I am working for the Socialites. The correct word would be *'with'*. I am as unhappy to work with them as they are to me.  This is a temporary placement - I am only working to gather information to relay back here." 

> "Finally, as little as I trust them, I want to highlight that *this* excursion may help ease tensions between the ARC and the Socialites. Perhaps they wont be as big a thorn in our side in the future."

__*PRELUDE*__

> "Across camp, I had noticed these fragments of a code plastered about. Foursquare, in specific. Managed to crack it and found a secondary code hidden inside as well." 


> "The code reads as follows:
> "Mirrors and smoke,
> All it seems to be.
> Yet here you are.
> 
> Grandest of illusions,
> Oppulence abundant,
> Destiny determined.
> 
> Here you stand,
> Above the wide field,
> Versus a mirror of self,
> Echoes of old fates
> 
> May his light shine,
> Eden echoes to all.
> Reaching upon you,
> Chills as he grasps,
> Your damned soul to blessed."


> "Of course - I originally hadn't the faintest idea *who* could have written these codes, but judging by the written text, I took my chances with the Socialites." 

> "And, as per usual, my hunch was right."

> "Don't know what I walked in on, but they were all gathered outside the Hart House with bags of kit, ready to mobilize. I counted **eleven** heads. When I got too close, however, one of the members named "Delaney" had stepped in my way to question me. At the time, I still wasn't sure if I was right, so I slipped in the codeword amongst a normal sentence. It didn't string up any reaction from Delaney, but it certainly did from Vivianne."

> "Turns out she was the one behind these codes. She had set them up around camp to "find someone capable" to act as their witness. There was no denying her obvious disappointment, I doubt they wanted an A.R.C. member amongst their ranks. *Why* they wanted a witness, I am unsure. I'm still yet to figure that out fully. For now, however, I am acting as their Journalist." 

> "Regardless, I was handed a rucksack similar to the ones those carried around me. Inside were camping supplies, and only camping supplies. Turns out we were headed into the woods for a two-to-three day long excursion. Only a scant-few agreed to join, myself included. Those joining were given emergency flares - for what reason, at the time, I was unaware."

> "The plan was simple; follow the coast past the swamp to head into the Birchwoods. We were hunting for Branchwalkers."

__*THE JOURNEY THERE*__

> "The journey was suprisingly pleasant."

> "However, the reason I'm writing this section is because of what Vivianne told us about "The Birch". 

> "The story is quite clear. Back in the days, spirit nights weren't *as* strong as the ones we currently experience. The Birch had this *catalyst* of-sorts to enhance the present of these Spirits. It was, as Vivianne said, as if they were infused by them. *That* is the reason why the forest is so deeply cherished by the Socialites. It's a gate to the other world -- a purgatory."

> "It wasn't long after that we eventually landed on a small clearing in the Birch Forest that overlooked the Swamp. This was going to be our set-up."

__*SETTING UP*__

> "Camp set-up went smoothly. I was suprised to see that a few of these Socialites were actually versed with the idea of Camping." 

> "The entie time we were there, however, a lone crow seemed to persistently watch us. Those who have had run-ins with the fabled "Birchwalker" are sure to recognise where this is going already, I'm sure."

> "Regardless, apart from a stray group of Dunkers coming to visit us, all went according to plan."

__*BRANCHWALKER SIGHTINGS*__

> "Now for tha part I'm suer you've been waiting to read."

> "Original sightings were scarce in value. Sightings were short, with the Branchwalkers oftentimes dissapearing after mere seconds. The first Branchwalker I spotted was one of Birch-make, tangled in fishing line and mushrooms. This was the Branchwalker that had prior followed us during the most recent A.R.C Recruitment." 

> "Soon enough, we saw a second one. This one was the one with the Crow. It had been injured from it's prior battle at the rodeo, with cracks running all across it's body. It's healing-strength is.... strong, to say the least. We saw one of the cracks practically shut itself over in front of us. What *fuels* their healing, I'm not sure. I had seen this Branchwalker before during the Bushman-Hunt. Our interaction was unfriendly, to say the least."

> "However, with the vast number of Branchwalkers active, I figured this was my one chance to conduct an experiment I've been *waiting* to do." 

> "For ease of writing, I will dub the Branchwalkers their names in order of sighting."
> - Net (The one caught in fishing line)
> - Odin (The one with the Crow) 

__*COMMUNICATION*__

> "Under Vivianne's watch, I approached Net, who had sprouted themselves from a nearby bush. I spoke the words "Solace the Forest" to it - a phrase that had been said to a few other witnesses in the past. I cautioned to the entity that I meant no harm, and that we were only here to assist and study them. Vivianne seemed surprised by my wording, as if she wasn't familiar of it herself." 

> "The moment I spoke those words, however, Odin sprouted itself in front of me as well."

> "At first, I thought they were being curious. But I was mistaken. They were watching me - as if what I had said attracted more of them onto our position. The woods only grew louder." 

> "I stepped forward towards Net, who remained in their spot prior, to try and further my communication. However, the closer I got, the stronger this feeling of dread seemed to wash over me. I could barely even hear my own thoughts, my fear was deafeningly loud."

> "The conversation was cut short - whether it was my choice or not, my body instinctively moved away from it." 

> "And then a third one showed itself." 

__*HEIMDALL*__

> "Something caught my eye - a set of three, multicolored dots in the far distance. I thought, originally, I had been seeing things, but it wasn't until I saw the tall, singular branch of a new Branchwalker sticking out from the bush in the far distance that I realised I was wrong." 

> "It was a Branchwalker of dark wood-make (perhaps spruce?). It had a two horns, one distinctly shorter than the other, which seemed to stretch upwards in the length of it's own body." 
 
> "It was waiting for me. That's not the paranoia talking, either -- I *knew* it was waiting for me. It's eyes were set on me, and me alone. I wanted to get closer, but my body was working against me."

> "I don't know what happened. My heart was going fast enough it had me worrying that I was going to have a *heart attack*. Yet the moment I thought I would, it stopped. A sense of relaxation like none other had hit me."

> "Looking back at the Branchwalker, it's watchful eye seemed to become... almost friendly. It was as if it didn't mean the harm it was causing." 

> "Before parting from it, I gave it a small offering of gathered Sage."

> "The experiment was a success."

__*POST-HEIMDALL SIGHTING*__

> "Once we turned away from it, Vivianne opened up to the crowd of Socialites cautioning not so stare at the Dark-Branchwalker. Apparently it "fucked with her head". Not me, though -- it seemed okay with my presence."

> "I would, however, extend this caution to those reading. I'm not sure of it's capabilities, nor it's friendliness to other Campers, and with a power *as* dangerous as mind manipulation, it's best to take caution." 


__*SWAMP VISIT*__

> "As per Socialite fashion, a bundle of them decided visiting the Swamps was the *best* idea. I stuck by them, as to make sure none of them would get lost or die whilst out there."

> "The swamps were... full. They couldn't see it, but I could feel the countless eyes that were watching us at once. I tried to warn them, but none of them were listening (as usual). Eventually, all three appeared before us. The moment they did, it felt like all the eyes of the forest were on us." 

> "We left soon after."

__*THEORIES AND NOTES*__

> "Been wondering if Odin's crow can work separately from it. It had appeared first, before the Branchwalker did, so perhaps it acts as a third eye." 

> "Heimdall has... some kind of mind manipulation abilities. In-fact, the majority of the Branchwalkers seem to persistently exhume this sense of dread and anxiety where they go, whether on purpose or not."

> "The phrase, Solace the Woods is more than just a request for help. I've used it as a means of opening communication with Branchwalkers and to show I'm not a threat to them. All reactions to my words appeared positive, albeit bringing more attention to myself than I had originally hoped for."

> "Vivianne seems to be only just learning about the Branchwalkers, too. She seems well versed, but lacks actual first-hand experience with them. Heimdall looks like it had an affect on her - it was enough for her to notice it's abilities, after all. Perhaps she isn't as connected as we thought - or perhaps the Branchwalkers (and maybe even the Bushman) are seperate from her. It's worth diving into, but still, remain respectful. At the end of the day, Vivianne is just a person like you and I."
