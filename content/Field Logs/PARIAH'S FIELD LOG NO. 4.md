## THE FOREST RAGES ON

__**GETTING TOO CLOSE**__
> "Don't know what led to everyone leaving the site, but I came back the moment everyone decided to funnel out. I could, for a moment, see Bushman wandering the forests ahead - only to then turn about to greet Evelyn who had just approached from behind. The conversation was short lasted, just something about *'returning to the party'*. It wasn't long however before Bushman walked up from behind Evelyn, glaring over her shoulder to look at me. **[1]**" 

> "The moment I acknowledged him being there, he turned to leave once more. I wasn't able to ask anything, let alone get a word out. It was then, afterwards, did everyone from ARC and a few from the party come running over. That's when shit hit the fan. Bushman warned us to leave, to which none of us did. It wasn't long before he brandished a spear, lunging forward to try and take down Marina." 

__**SHIT HITS THE FAN**__
> "Luckily, Marina was pulled out the way in time to avoid the strike. Several other campers, now noticing the commotion, ran over to try and fight Bushman (???), which soon warranted the appearance of Odin. Something I noticed, however, is the additional crow he now carries on a separate branch. **[2]** Before, he had one. Now a second one joins it. It really feels as if they're filling out the name "Odin" now. Two crows: Huginn and Muninn." 

> "Aida came around to carry me off - Don't know what happened to me, but I froze up. Soon enough, the rest of the ARC followed after too, and we made our evacuation to one of the nearby cabins." 

__**HUNKERING DOWN**__
> "Honestly... We kind of said fuck it to staying there. Or, atleast I did."

> "Aida and I broke out through one of the windows in the opposing rooms."

__**POSSIBLE PEACE?**__
> "After a long time of walking, Aida and I landed ourselves at a fork in the path. When deciding which way to take, Bushman walked *directly* across from us. **[3]** I tried to follow after him - ask why this was happening, but he didn't listen. I followed him, just for a moment, before losing him. Though, it wasn't long before he showed himself again. Not even ten feet away from me. Just... watching. Staring at Aida and myself." **[4]**

> "He left afterwards. But he didn't kill us - didn't even lay a finger on us. It was like we were excluded from his efforts of attacking every camper he could." 

__**CLOSING**__
> "It wasn't long before the fog cleared up."

> "There isn't much to say - barely any notes to take note of. Expect it to be short."

__**NOTES**__
> "The woods are growing louder and *angrier*. We've never seen a result like this for lingering too close to somewhere we shouldn't have been. It's *that*, or the Stonehenges are important enough to be killed over. I don't know where Odin's second grow came from, but it feels like things are only going downhill."
