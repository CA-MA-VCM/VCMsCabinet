## Preface
> This field log spans two days, as it was an ongoing investigation into the disappearance of Rupert, a jock. He is safe and sound.

## Day One — Initial Disappearance
> During a trip to the library, I found some high ground to give the church a brief check. A crowd had formed outside it (Theodore, Radly, others), so I ushered them away from the church and asked them why they were there. Rupert had disappeared, and their search led them there (to no avail.)
> The following details were uncovered:
> - Rupert, a jock, was taken by a spirit.
> - This spirit was described as having short hair, glasses, a striped shirt, and looking kind of rich. This description fits that of a Girn, minus the short hair.
> - He was apparently "grabbed." This initially led me to assume a wraith was behind this, but this proved to be untrue.

> From here, I joined OWL and ELPIS at the library where we ran into Miss Teen America, a poltergeist. We interviewed her, both about the disappearance of Rupert and herself.
> - She does not know much about other spirits.
> - Claimed to not know anything about the disappearance of Rupert.
> - Her shapeshifting isn't actually shapeshifting. It's a form of illusion, by bending the light. Bright lights (such as sunlight) cause it to not function.
>   - She showed this off by making herself look like the Girn mentioned prior.
> - Did not know of any spirit that frequented the church.
> - Asked me to find out her 'real name', which she does not know herself. Said that if I do, we can 'really talk.'

## Day Two — Rupert's Reappearance
> I spotted Rupert at the cafeteria, and began asking questions. He's safe and sound, but his testimony seems to contradict what I'd heard prior. Here's a rundown of what I learnt of the interview.
> - Described the spirit; It was clearly Miss Teen America
> - She was originally opaque, and had a gray glow.
>   - Likely her light bending abilities
> - At some point in this ordeal made him run a marathon.
>   - She hates guys. This is, according to the interview, genuinely just why she did this.
>   - Theodore argued in favor of Rupert multiple times, as Miss Teen America had threatened him several times ("Stop running, and I'll kill you.")
> - Asked if she's pretty.
> - Asked "you, or your friend?" Rupert answered himself.
> - Moved to shade to 'transform.'
> - Despite Radly's testimony, Rupert was not physically grabbed; it was done through telekinesis.
> - Rupert claims to have been teleported. This is unlikely, and other testimonies seem to imply he was flown off instead. Perhaps he went unconscious?
> - Queen Georgia claims Miss Teen America holds the ability to possess.
