# "Theodore's Glowing Eyes."
*Is this what it looks like for them to change?*

## Conditions & Other
- *TIME:* Afternoon
- *LOCATION*: Group A (Cafeteria)
- *WEATHER*: Clear skies
- *WITNESSES:* Crowd of campers. Among them were Izra, Simon, and Cub.

## Phenomena
- Spirit sighting
- Unusual spirit behavior
- Manipulation of electronics (lamps, Walkman, Scovinski's computer)
- Phasing into a computer (?)
- Bursts of light
- Emitting static
- Glowing eyes (on the spirit)

## Preface
> Nothing seemed to 'summon' him, or y'know, inflict this behavior. He showed up like this in the middle of a crowded cafeteria.

## Description of Events
> Theodore, a known spirit, appeared in the cafeteria near the balcony. His eyes were glowing, it was kind of hard to look at. Through this entire thing, he did not speak a single word.
> He began messing with the cafeteria lights, making them flicker rapidly before turning off. When they *did* come back on, they were a deep red. Like, stereotypical evil red. He messed with people's radios, and cassette players too. Anything that could play music or audio at all began to spout a really loud static.

> One of the campers tried to interact with him, and he began glowing really, REALLY bright in response. It was blinding. He appeared behind one of us, made a loud static noise and then did it again after another camper pissed him off. Outside, he approached Scovinski, and flew into her office after. Chasing him there was pretty difficult. I had to like, climb onto the roof of the porch to spy through the window.

> I couldn't see or hear a lot, and they eventually closed the curtains, but before that I saw him fly into the computer. Scovinski looked really upset by it, so I think he really messed it up. Counselors *did* go and get me off of the roof, but nothing really came of it.

> At the end of it, Theodore flew up into the sky before being struck by lightning, despite the clear skies. Then, he was gone.

## Notes & Theories
> My only guess is that Theodore is in the process of becoming a poltergeist. His 'spores' were still blue, but I don't know how long that'll stay, and he was manipulating things in ways normal ghosts tend not to.
> Everything he did was also 'triggered' by people pissing him off, but that might just be Theo. He was kind of a spiteful bastard.
