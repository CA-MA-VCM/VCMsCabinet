## __THE RODEO.__

` 10/11/1987 - EVENING - THE RODEO`
`WITNESSES: Evelyn, Marina, Cal, Indie, Kacy + Many Campers`  

### Leadup to the Rodeo.
> The Rodeo was announced a few days ago, which seemed to get quite a lot of excitement with many Campers. On the day itself, most people wore their Rodeo outfit the entire day, seeming to be very excited about the event. Interactions I had throughout the day seemed that most people were quite optimistic about the event, and no one really concerned about if something went wrong.

### The Rodeo itself.
> The Rodeo itself was very crowded. Many people came over to have some fun. People queued for the Bull, even myself and Evelyn did that. Nothing really felt out of place, and there was no sense of ... impending doom. Everyone was having fun. Of course there was a few injuries from people on the real and mechanical bulls. Everyone was having fun though. 
> 
> A little while into the event, animals also started showing up. It seems that this is a regular occurrence whenever there is a crowd. Again, unfortunately most people's reactions to the animals were to feed them, scare them or shoo them off. However I am relieved that I at least didn't see anyone getting violent with the animals, in comparison to a few days ago. The animals didn't stay for long though, eventually fleeing off back into the woods.
> 
> Then gnomes turned up. It was quite surprising to me. At this point I had heard about many of the different creatures or entities that live amongst us in Camp Astrid, but gnomes weren't one of them. I noticed more fascination with the gnomes then with the animals. However, I got a bit distracted after Evelyn decided to ... *kick* a gnome. I don't really understand why, especially as Evelyn knows the risks of hurting the creatures that live among us more then other people. However I won't go into more detail about it. The gnomes eventually fled, leaving the rodeo to continue without any other disruptions. Or that's what we thought.

> ### The Earthquake
> Right before the Earthquake, the Bull had escaped the pen, fleeing up the mountainside. It must have only been a few moments after that which the earth began to shake. Me, Kacy, Evelyn, Simon and a few others fled the scene, staying a safe distance. Most of our campers followed suit, fleeing the scene, although not running too far. It was only a few minutes after that when the entire cliffside collapsed on the rodeo. Everyone was terrified, clinging onto one another, watching in horror. I believe no one was crushed under the rockfall.
> 
> We returned back to see the damage. It was all gone. I followed behind other campers that had decided to climb up to get a better view. That's when I saw them. Three figures standing off in the distance, watching what was going on. I couldn't hear what they were saying, I did not want to approach. However all I'm left with is questions. Who are they? What do they know? It's best not to think about it for now. They only stayed for a moment, before leaving.
> 
> Everyone was still terrified, people were scrambled. Marina called out to my fellow ARC members to look for them all before leaving, so that's what we did. It wasn't my... intention to get separated. We were a big crowd so I thought it be fine to split off from Kacy and Evelyn to go look for fellow ARC members that might have been on the rockslide itself. I couldn't find anyone, and when I turned back, the crowd had disappeared.
> 
> I saw people in the distance, so I went in that direction, trying to keep up with anyone around me so I wouldn't be alone. From here on out, I can't report on much of what the general crowd was feeling or doing, due to my situation. I kept running in the same direction until it lead me to the road...

> ### The Amber man
> When I approached the road, I saw him, what I believed to be The Amber man. He was... taller then I expected. He was stood in front of a crowd by the tunnel, and I was cut off from that crowd as I'm not stupid enough to dash past. I fled to the side, watching what had unfolded behind a few rocks, which I believed to be safe enough distance where I wouldn't be noticed. That's when I saw it. Amber man picking Ria up, and throwing her into one of the Branch walkers that were there. She must have died on the spot... or at least I hope she did.
> 
> I watched as the Branch Walker tried to scare off the other campers. It was too much of a distance to hear or see exactly what they were doing in response. A few of them didn't leave, including my friend Victoria. They wanted to grab Ria's body, putting their own lives on the line to recover it. I watched as they did just that and I watched how the Branch walker and the Amber man fought.
> 
> At that point the crowd had fled, so I did the same, running along the path towards the bridge. Victoria, with a few others I'm not familiar with were there, holding Ria's body. I stayed there for a moment, again I didn't want to run off further by myself, nor did I want Victoria to stay and get hurt. I was keeping an eye on my surroundings, checking my back, and that's when I noticed it.
> 
> I saw Amber man in the distance, on the main bridge. It seemed like there was Branch walkers there too, but it was quite a distance and I couldn't tell. All I could see is that there was some sort of... confrontation. I however decided to run after watching for a moment. I didn't want to end up like Ria. I kept following a group, going with them into a Cabin until it was safe to come out.

> ### Conclusion
> We have yet to check out exactly what happened. Nor do I think... me or many of my friends wish to do that right now. Amber man seems to be more of a threat, and we are left with un-answered questions on why the Branch walkers and Bushman were protecting us. Everyone seems shaken up and a bit more on edge after the rodeo.
