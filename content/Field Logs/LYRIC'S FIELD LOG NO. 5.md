> ***Setting***: Group D, Night time, maybe 9 pm
> ***Anomalies spotted***: 
> - Bushman 
> - Branchwalkers: Odin, Net

# ***TIMELINE***:

> "Took place last night after the basketball game, I already had my doubts about having such a bit group near the forest, but even when things got actually loud everything seemed fine. It wasn't until after Counselor Dane left the area that we saw them. 
> 
> I was near the tree line with Coyote when a mutual friend of ours took a picture of us, which meant he saw what was behind us. Upon showing up the photo, me and Coyote both noticed the danger, and notified our higher up of the issue. I tried to notify the crowd of the imminent danger but of course, as usual, I was disregarded. We kept watch of the forest until the sun went down"

***CROWD CONTROL***:

> "Maybe half an hour after the initial sighting of the Bushman, the second sighting, as well as the actual clearing out of the area, began. Bushman seemed to ignore the crowd for once, instead traveling with two of his Branchwalkers, later identified as Odin and Net, seemingly investigating where ever the Hollow man had been the night before. 
> 
> Of course, as they usually do, the crowd at the game wanted to follow. Luckily for us, a myriad of people actually managed to control the crowd and keep them back. Thankfully, for once, people actually listened and stayed back, heading more back towards the tennis court and speaking amongst themselves. 
> 
> As for myself, I was dragged off to a cabin in D group for shelter, though I did make my way back for a head count since a couple in the group I was in we're missing. What I came back to should've been expected"

***IDIOCRACY***:

> "I came back to the group to find someone, identified as one of the people responsible for the bounty I put up (Vorg), taking pictures of the Bushman and nearby Branchwalkers. This seemed to set him off, he walked over to the man, and stabbed through his camera, tossing it to the side, and belting out an order to leave. 
> 
> I along with my group left when he said, though others weren't as obedient. A few stragglers stayed around him, to which the Bushman started to attack, stabbing one camper before the rest scattered, myself included. We followed Victoria back to her cabin and boarded the place up before taking refuge in the closet.
> 
> We would've stayed there indefinitely if it weren't for outside forces complicating things"

***BREAK IN***:

> "Whilst we were shacked up in the cabin, unbeknownst to me at the time, the two Branchwalkers Bushman was with had come to our cabin. For reasons unknown to me, they broke a window, but did not enter. My best guess is that they we're trying to see what we'd do, though the reasoning for that is still unknown. I did a sweep of the house after I was sure they had gone, and confirmed that it was indeed Branchwalkers that did it. 
> 
> I went back to hiding, but it was short lived. There was once again another break in, this time however from the Bushman himself. He didn't leave though after breaking the window, no he came straight to the closet e were in, broke the door, and got right to questioning. 
> 
> He asked Victoria how she got hurt, and she answered. I think if she said anything but what he wanted to know, he would've killed her. Luckily she obeyed, that's what we all did right, we listened and he left us alone. Now, after his questionnaire, he left, and as far as I know, he went right back to the forest afterwards. 

***CONCLUSION***:
> "There's one big thing I think we can gleam from this, and that's that the Bushman is investigating it. Sam had a good saying for this, "Enemy of my Enemy", we need to meet with him, we need to tell him we're against the Hollow man too, and we need to get him on our side. Or at least make it to where we can investigate without him interrupting"
