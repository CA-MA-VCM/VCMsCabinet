**Date:** 
> 12/28/87

**Location:** 
> Just outside Group B, By Georgia's Cabin

**Witness(es):** 
> PROCTOR, FREKI, Imogen and some of her people, campers

**Phenomenon:** 
> Poltergeist(?)

**What Happened:**
> A poltergeist (assumed) appear, without warning. Nothing seemed to summon her. Poltergeist was a woman with blonde hair. 60's style attire and hairstyle with a "meat/eyeball" face similar to those interacted with in the past, glows yellow. She asked campers to sing "Daisy Bell," mostly Imogen. Imogen happily sang along, being familiar with this spirit in particular. PROCTOR hummed along and another camper was asked to sing, all of which pleased the spirit. She then asked Imogen if she believe her, her friends, and campers would survive and leave camp. She replied with yes. After some banter, the spirit left.

**Reactions:**
> Proctor observed while Imogen did most of the interacting. She seemed very familiar with this one in particular. The rest were curious, observing on the sidelines. None got close out of caution other than Imogen. Another camper was asked to sing, and did so.

**Theory or Notes:**
> Though I am still learning a bit, using what knowledge I know before hand, I was under the initial impression that Poltergeist were ones typically confused or unstable, believing they either they are alive still or that the living were spirits.
> This one, however, seemed to both acknowledge the living as the living and seemed to understand its own existence as well, though I could be wrong and she was just really, really ominous or confusing with her statements.
> If I'm wrong in my initial assumptions on poltergeist, my bad. I'm just using what I know already beforehand.
> She seemed pleased with just one or two people singing. Makes me wonder what would happen if no one did.
> Imogen has claimed to befriend several spirits, and now I'm believing that to be more true the more I interact with her.
