Ghosts are the most common type of [[Spirits/index|Spirit]], recognisable by blue spores and a blue glow. Under most circumstances, they cannot interact with the material world, only occasionally through light and indirect ways such as messing with electronics or accelerating plant growth.

Ghosts are, in most cases, the initial stage of spirithood. Spirits are not guaranteed to become [[Spirits/B - Poltergeists/index|Poltergeists]] or [[Spirits/C - Wraiths/index|Wraiths]], as they may move on before this, but [[Spirits/B - Poltergeists/index|Poltergeists]] and [[Spirits/C - Wraiths/index|Wraiths]] were usually ghosts at first. This 'progression' can take anywhere from two to thirty years according to [[Harrison]], but in [[Johnny Boy|one particular case]] a Spirit has made an appearance as a Wraith immediately.

Not all ghosts' backgrounds need to be actively researched, as their deaths and history might be entirely mundane. If they seem to just be normal people — which most are — then it's just plain rude. You should always prioritize keeping them comfortable and being friendly over 'gathering info.'

Out of all three Spirit types, Ghosts seem to be the most mentally stable.

# Common Anomalous Capabilities

Most ghosts share a few capabilities.

## Flight

Ghosts tend to fly around whenever they feel like it, and have great control over this technique. They might use it to escape a situation, or just to show off.

## Phasing

Often used in tandem with flight, they can simply phase through walls and other surfaces.

# Uncommon Anomalous Capabilities

There's a few less common, shared ones as well.

## Mindreading

Often unintentional, they may hear your thoughts as if you spoke them out loud. Some ghosts are aware of this, some aren't and believe you're speaking normally.

## Invisibility

Simply put; the ability to go invisible. They may still talk during this.
