Wraiths are the third type of [[Spirits/index|Spirit]], and are indicated by a red glow, red spores, and radio static louder than that of a [[Spirits/Poltergeists/index|Poltergeist]]. They share every ability held by [[Spirits/Poltergeists/index|Poltergeists]], but are able to interact with the world far more directly, even in the absence of Shades such as [[Blueshrooms]] or spore nights. They are tethered to our world by hate and malice, and act purely out of these emotions.

Very little is known about these, other than that they're incredibly dangerous to be around. Judging by the pattern of crumbling mental states in the progression from [[Spirits/Ghosts/index|Ghost]] to [[Spirits/Poltergeists/index|Poltergeist]], it's fair to assume it's even worse here. Proceed with extreme caution.
