Johnny Boy is the first [[Spirits/Wraiths/index|Wraith]] we've ever become aware of. Despite wraiths usually taking about two to thirty years to form (from initially being [[Spirits/Ghosts/index|Ghosts]]), Johnny became a wraith immediately.

# Behavior

Unknown, so far. But presumably quite violent. He was a violent man in life, firing up the Socialites v Dunkers war and trying to burn the Hart house in an attempt to kill Vivianne.

# Background

Johnny Boy was an adult living within Camp Astrid for an extended period of time. Presumably, he was a camper in 1977, at which he seems to have killed someone. Upon this information being public, he was shot by Greene Girn himself out in the woods.
