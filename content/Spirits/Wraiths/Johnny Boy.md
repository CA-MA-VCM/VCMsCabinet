Johnny Boy is the first [[Spirits/Wraiths/index|Wraith]] we've ever become aware of. Despite wraiths usually taking about two to thirty years to form (from initially being [[Spirits/Ghosts/index|Ghosts]]), Johnny became a wraith immediately.

Radly has requested we do not [[Sealing|seal]] him, unless absolutely necessary. We intend to honour this request.

# Behavior

Unknown, so far. But presumably quite violent. He was a violent man in life, firing up the Socialites v Dunkers war on the side of the dunkers and trying to burn the Hart house in an attempt to kill Vivianne.

# Appearance

Unknown.

# Anomalies

Unknown.

# Background

Johnny Boy was an adult living within Camp Astrid for an extended period of time. Presumably, he was a camper in 1977, at which he seems to have killed someone. Upon this information being public, he was shot by Greene Girn himself out in the woods.

Before this, he worked extensively with the Dunkers, even saving Radly's life on one occasion. If you wish to research this man's background, you should contact Radly.
