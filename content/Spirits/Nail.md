# Behavior

As far as behavior goes, Nail is harder to read than any other entity, human or otherwise. He's been know to act both friendly and hostile towards campers and ghosts alike, and seemingly goes between the two at his leisure. As far as why he does what he does, he himself has explained that he does it for sport. He's described hunting various mythical creatures such as "Bigfoot", "Aliens", and even "Werewolves". As far as for hunting people and [[Spirits/index|ghosts]], he's described hunting them as "Boring". Aside from that, he feels everything a human can, and does things based on that as well, for example, when asked too many questions, he may get annoyed and get violent. 

# Physique

The bushman is a human ([[Spirits/index|Spirit]]) standing at about 6'8, has a large build and stature. For clothing, he wears dark, makeshift clothing as well as a poncho, and a Stetson hat with metal protrusions surrounding it. Covering his face is a type of metal mask, what exactly it is is unknown, but whatever it is makes him unable to see. Around his waist is a large knife, the implications of such is obvious. 

# Anomalies
The main anomaly is that he's a powerful spirit in general. It's already rare enough that he can interact physically with the living world

## 'Ashing'

One of his abilities unique to him is his ability to "Ash" people. How he does this isn't known, but what is does to people is. Upon touching someone, or by simply directing it himself, a person that he afflicts will first get surrounded by Ash, before turning into it themselves. Upon which, the individual affected will be transported somewhere else without much harm, other than a nasty headache.
