Ghosts are the most common type of [[Spirits/index|Spirit]], recognisable by blue spores and a blue glow. Under most circumstances, they cannot interact with the material world, only occasionally through light and indirect ways such as messing with electronics or accelerating plant growth.

Ghosts are, in most cases, the initial stage of spirithood. Spirits are not guaranteed to become [[Spirits/Poltergeists/index|Poltergeists]] or [[Spirits/Wraiths/index|Wraiths]], as they may move on before this, but [[Spirits/Poltergeists/index|Poltergeists]] and [[Spirits/Wraiths/index|Wraiths]] were usually ghosts at first. This 'progression' can take anywhere from two to thirty years according to [[Harrison]], but in [[Johnny Boy|one particular case]] a Spirit has made an appearance as a Wraith immediately.

Not all ghosts' backgrounds need to be actively researched. If they seem to just be normal people — which most are — then it's just plain rude. You should always prioritize keeping them comfortable and being friendly over 'gathering info.'

Out of all three Spirit types, Ghosts seem to be the most mentally stable.
