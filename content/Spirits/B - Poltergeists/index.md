Poltergeists are the second type of [[Spirits/index|Spirit]], indicated by a yellow glow, and yellow spores. Unlike [[Spirits/A - Ghosts/index|Ghosts]], these spirits can interact with the world, usually by throwing things around with telekinesis, but never directly with their own hands. These spirits are often incredibly confused, and affect the world by complete accident in most cases.

Despite having telekinetic abilities, they do not actually seem to pick up on the fact that it's telekinetic if they have not realised their own death yet. Instead, they're either oblivious to it, or haven't actually made the connection yet that what they're doing isn't 'normal.'

Judging by [[Harrison]], these spirits are quite unstable. Do not under any circumstances agitate them, as they are very much capable of hurting you in an emotional outburst.

# Common Anomalous Capabilities

First off, poltergeists almost always inherit the [[Spirits/A - Ghosts/index#Common Anomalous Capabilities|common anomalous capabilities]] of [[Spirits/A - Ghosts/index|Ghosts]], as well as having a higher chance to have [[Spirits/A - Ghosts/index#Mindreading|mindreading]] capabilities.

## Telekinesis 

Using their mind, they are able to throw around objects, on purpose or due to an emotional outburst of some kind.
