Harrison, also known as 'Devil Dog,' is the [[Spirits/B - Poltergeists/index|Poltergeist]] of a ghost investigator. He has about two decades of experience under his belt, and judging by his WW1 trench knife, died somewhere in the first half of the century.

# Behavior

Harrison is a quite unstable poltergeist, yet incredibly cooperative. Believing us to be junior ghost investigators ourselves, he is willing to divulge just about any information you ask of him, as long as you remain respectful. This is unfortunately where the main issue lies. Harrison is not aware of his death, and it is ==absolutely vital== that you keep this charade up. We're the ghosts, not him, and we're new deaths hoping to learn about ourselves. Anything that might tip him off to being dead throws him into an unstable spiral.

On top of that, he has memory issues, and realizing he doesn't remember something he should throws him into that same distress that suggesting he's dead does. Below is a list of things *not* to ask about. This list may be updated overtime.

- His 'crew'.
- Who the 'best ghost hunter is', as he considers himself the second best.
- His death.
- His past.
- Anything about his spirithood, such as telekinetic abilities or mindreading.

Further, he also appears to have somewhat an ego about being a ghost investigator. He believes nothing gets past him, and you should never imply anything has.

# Appearance

You'll immediately notice the many eyes on the side of his face; This is his most notable visual aspect. Other than that, he wears a wide brim hat and a trench coat. He also sports a belt, with a whole bunch of ghost hunting gear and a trench knife, dated 1917.

# Anomalies & Abilties

## Unusual Poltergeist Ability Usage

He is a fairly standard poltergeist, and does seem to use telekinetic abilities quite often, but without realizing it. This isn't to say it happens by accident, however—he uses them willingly, but does not realize they're telekinetic powers. If he uses it to tip over your chair, he'll probably think he just did it with his hands.

Do not point this out. Maybe he just never truly connected that he does these things without touching them, rather than think he is manipulating them with his hands. A similar thing applies to his [[Spirits/A - Ghosts/index#Mindreading|mindreading capabilities]]; He'll think you just said it.

## Minor Shapeshifting

He has once been observed turning all of his 'eyes' to teeth, and licking them with a large tongue. During this, he said "I WONDER HOW IT TASTES." referring to the [[Amberman]].

## Trench Shotgun

This isn't an anomaly as much as it's something to keep note of; he has a trench shotgun hidden under his coat.

# Background

As far as we know, he was a soldier in the first world war. After going home, he travelled to the Crystal Valley to investigate ghosts with his crew. He did this for about twenty years, before dying. Cause of death is currently unknown.
