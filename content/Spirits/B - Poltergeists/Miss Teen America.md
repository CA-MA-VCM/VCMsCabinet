Miss Teen America is a [[Spirits/B - Poltergeists/index|Poltergeist]] resembling a teen beauty pageant winner. Unlike some other spirits, she is aware of her death and appears to have made relative peace with it. Still though, do not press this issue without a good reason; most [[Spirits/index|Spirits]] do not appreciate it.

# Behavior

Miss Teen America is energetic, and acts kind of posh. For a poltergeist, she's fairly stable; you won't feel like you're tiptoeing around a rule set like with [[Harrison]], although you still want to avoid upsetting her. She loves hearing and talking about herself, and matching her upbeat energy seems to do wonders. Importantly though, she absolutely hates guys. She regularly bothers them, makes them do meaningless chores, and on one occasion even kidnapped one using telekinesis. Not only that, but she has a record of making them go on "death runs"; horribly long marathons under the threat of death.

If you're a guy and wish to research this poltergeist, you might want to consider cross dressing.

# Appearance

Miss Teen America sports a blue dress, a leopard print boa, a few pearl necklaces, and a tiara. Furthermore, she also has incredibly pale gray-ish skin and some type of shadow covering her eyes.

# Anomalous Capabilities

Aside from holding all common [[Spirits/B - Poltergeists/index|Poltergeist]] capabilities, as well as [[Spirits/A - Ghosts/index#Invisibility|invisibility]], she holds a few more seen below.

## Illusions

While it might appear to be shape shifting at first, it is actually an illusionary ability based on bending the light. They've seemingly perfected this, but it does not work in broad daylight; she has to be indoors, or in the shade. According to a report, she's used this to hide the fact that she's a spirit at all, causing her to appear opaque.

## Possession

MTA appears to be able to possess people, but has only promised to do this as one end of a deal, asking two people to 'sell their souls' to her in order to let her possess them. It is unknown how literal this is, but it does mean ELPIS and OWL are currently at severe risk of possession.

# Background

Not much is known about her background, or even how old she is. She's obviously a beauty pageant winner, but not much is known about her death or why she hates guys to the extent that she does. She herself does not even know her real name.
