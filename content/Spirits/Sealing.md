Sealing is, presumably, a ritual of some kind used to seal away [[Spirits/index|Spirits]], either forcing them to move on or locking them away to never be seen again. The specifics of this are unknown, but it was mentioned to be an option by [[Harrison]]. Unfortunately, he believes we have no need to learn how to seal as he believes we are ghosts ourselves, and would not be able to seal other ghosts.
