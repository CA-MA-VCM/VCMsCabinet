The Amberman, also known as "Woodsman" or "Hollow Man", is a large tree-like creature standing at about eighteen feet tall. It is actively hostile and ==extremely== dangerous, as it actively hunts campers for food.

## Physique

The Amberman appears to be incredibly sturdy, even shrugging off bullets. Despite being made of wood, it's inner "tar" douses flames, making this a non-option.

## Anomalies

The Amberman's main feat is its sheer size and strength. Despite that, it still holds two anomalies to its name.

### Regeneration

It boasts unnatural regenerative capabilities, fueled by its eating. This has been seen healing wounds in seconds, and may be part of why he eats so much.

### Fog

Its appearances are often accompanied by fog. If you're out somewhere and fog rolls in, that might be time to get into a building.

> [!danger] WARNING
> A lack of fog does *not* mean it has left. Do not let this lure you into a false sense of safety.
> Likewise, fog does not *always* mean it will appear.
