### Description
The Crooked Shaman is a mysterious, unknown entity that presents itself after continued usage of the [[Blossoms]]. Very, very little is known about The Crooked Shaman, asides from the fact it has found itself in the minds of multiple people. 

Those afflicted by The Shaman are:

- Calliope Chambers
- An Unnamed [[Hellbillies|Hellbilly]]. (FORMERLY)
- [[Sackhead]].

However, despite sharing the affliction, no visions of the Shaman are the same. For instance, a [[Hellbillies|Hellbilly]] saw The Crooked Shaman as a man in red clothes, a black rubber apron who donned a sack over his head. Calliope, on the other hand, frequently views The Shaman as a humanoid figure made out of pure, amalgamated flesh. 

### Dangers
Along the psychological danger that comes with frequent visions, The Crooked Shaman becomes significantly more dangerous once the afflicted has fallen asleep. It is here, in their dreams, that The Shaman can attack them. In the event that they are caught - or killed - in their dreams, the afflicted will undergo a seizure. 

Alongside this, those affected by The Shaman may find themselves falling into bouts of narcolepsy, forcing them to confront the Entity. This, as a result, often forces the afflicted to stay awake for long periods of time, which can propose a significant health-risk if done too frequently. 

Visions of The Shaman can appear in consciousness, but has shown no ability to attack the afflicted as of writing. 

### Counters
Countering, or overcoming The Crooked Shaman, is no easy feat. According to the afflicted Hellbilly, one can subdue the Entity through prolonged meditation or mental-fortification. Asides from this, no other counters have been found.
