Mothlight is a [[Mediums|Medium]] with the ability to possess and influence others through his powers.
# Behavior

Mothlight is one of the most directly or indirectly hostile towards campers and most other humans. In most of his interactions with others, he has been shown to be narcissistic and malevolent towards others. As it stands, Mothlight is the person that we know the least about, so far his interactions have solely been him trying to harm others, either that, or intimidate and instill fear in others. 

## Motives

Once again, as far as his real motives go, it isn't completely known. Nail has stated his to be a part of the Ignition Front, but he has also been seen attacking the Front directly. Even after his death, he has been shown taunting campers without any real goal in mind. 

# Physique

"Mothlight", as he is known, seems to be a human male standing at about 5'5, with an overall average build. Similar to [[Nail]] and [[Bushman]], not much of what he actually looks like is unknown, with his face and any other descriptive features being covered by clothing. As for clothing, Mothlight wears a black cloak and robes that cover his entire body. On his arms and neck are a multitude of bracelets and necklaces, each one having some sort of trinket or tooth hanging from them. Covering his face is a white mask with a large split down the middle, and behind it, seemingly covering his face, are bandages. On his back, lays a large cleaver like sword that he seems to use sparingly. 

# Anomalies

Mothlights main ability, a sort of mind control, stems from him being a [[Mediums|Medium]]. As of right now however, his abilities are much more limited. 

## Mind Control

As it stands it's fairly self explanatory, he has been able to enter or otherwise affect people's minds in a way that makes them do what he wishes against their will. Though, it has been described as "Mind Control" in a literal sense, and more as him replacing their personalities with his own, making the original person more of a passenger in their own body. 

## Gate Manipulation

On multiple occasions now, Mothlight has been able to open or simply step through [[Stonehenges]] without them necessarily having to be completely activated.

# Background

He's known to have revived himself at least once, according to [[Nail]].
